## LinkedIn

The IEEPA tariff refund fight just narrowed to one question: will the Federal Circuit stay Judge Eaton's universal refund order?

Where things stand this morning:

• CBP has collected ~$166B in IEEPA duties. ~$85B is accepted into the CAPE refund pipeline; $20B+ has already gone out the door.
• On June 2 DOJ appealed both the universal refund order and the order compelling Commissioner Scott to testify.
• The Federal Circuit stayed the testimony order — but Judge Eaton sent a substitute Customs witness, and the June 9 show-cause hearing went forward.
• Crucially, Eaton denied CBP's motion to disclaim authority over finally liquidated entries. The universal scope stands — for now.

The Supreme Court already decided the merits in February (Learning Resources v. Trump). This appeal is about remedy: how far the refund order reaches and whether the oldest entries are in or out.

What that means if you paid IEEPA duties: do not wait for the appeal. Protest deadlines run 180 days from liquidation regardless of appellate posture. File before any stay can interrupt CBP's intake.
