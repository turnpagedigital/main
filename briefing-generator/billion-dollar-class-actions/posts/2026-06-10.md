# $1B+ Class Actions & Mass Arbitration — Social Posts | June 10, 2026

## LinkedIn

The biggest matters of this litigation cycle are all converting at once — from "contested" to "implement and distribute."

Three this week:

• Visa/Mastercard interchange: Judge Brian Cogan granted preliminary approval to a settlement built to finally end 21 years of litigation. ~12 million merchants. The new deal leads with structure, not just cash: a 10-bps cut to average credit interchange for 5 years, a 1.25% cap on standard consumer cards for 8 years, and expanded surcharging rights. The separate $5.5B damages fund is already paying out.

• Roundup at the Supreme Court: Monsanto v. Durnell was argued April 27 on whether FIFRA preempts state failure-to-warn claims. A decision is expected before the term ends this month — and it will reshape the exposure math on 100,000+ glyphosate claims overnight.

• Purdue/Sackler: the $7.4B opioid settlement went effective May 1. After Harrington killed nonconsensual third-party releases, this is the proof that a consent-based structure can still deliver a global resolution.

For claimants the message is timing. Preliminary approval starts the clock. A Supreme Court opinion can drop any day. Be positioned before the deadline, not after.
