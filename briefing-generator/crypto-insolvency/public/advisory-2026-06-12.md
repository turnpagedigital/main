### I. FTX Recovery Trust — Fifth Distribution Mechanics, June 16 Record Date, and the $600M Reserve Reduction

The FTX Trading Ltd. bankruptcy (Bankr. D. Del., No. 22-11068, Hon. John T. Dorsey, Courtroom 5, 824 N. Market St., Wilmington, DE) has now completed four distribution rounds totaling approximately $10 billion in the aggregate. The FTX Recovery Trust has set a **June 16, 2026** record date for the next distribution to holders of allowed claims and interests, with distributions expected to commence **July 31, 2026**. Payments to eligible Preferred Equity Holders are likewise scheduled for July 31, and holders of Allowed NFT Customer Entitlement Claims under the confirmed Chapter 11 Plan will become eligible to initiate the NFT distribution process beginning **June 30, 2026** (__[CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/ftx-creditor-payouts-july-2026/)__).

The reserve mechanics are consequential: as part of the ongoing claims reconciliation process, the Trust filed an amended notice with the Bankruptcy Court seeking approval to reduce the disputed claims reserve by approximately **$600 million**, decreasing the reserve from $2.4 billion to $1.8 billion. That reserve compression is what funds the incremental July 31 payout tranche. Creditors should treat June 16 as a hard cutoff: all customers and creditors must complete required pre-distribution procedures — including KYC verification, tax documentation, and onboarding with an approved service provider — before distributions can be processed; for holders of transferred claims, distributions will only be made where the transfer is fully processed and reflected on the official claims register as of the June 16 record date and the applicable 21-day objection period has expired without objection.

The cumulative recovery trajectory by class is well-documented through the March 31 fourth round. The fourth distribution — approximately $2.2 billion released March 31 — brought many customer claim classes, including U.S. customers in Class 5B and Classes 6A and 6B, to 100% recovery, while Class 7 reached a cumulative 120%. Class 5A Dotcom claimants received an additional 18% increment (bringing total recovery to 96%), while Classes 6A and 6B each received a 15% increment to full recovery. Distributions across all rounds now total roughly $10 billion since distributions began in early 2025.

The preferred equity tranche warrants separate attention. The estate set April 30, 2026 as the record date for its first payments to preferred equity holders, with payments scheduled for May 29, 2026; eligible holders must complete ownership certification, KYC verification, and tax documentation to qualify. The July 31 round will carry the second preferred equity payment. The Trust has also been running a parallel adversarial posture: Genesis Digital Assets — a bitcoin mining company — is fighting a lawsuit brought by the FTX estate seeking roughly $1 billion, with Genesis pushing to dismiss and rejecting the legal grounds for the alleged pre-collapse transfers. Oral argument for Adversary Proceeding No. 24-50222 was calendared for April 8, 2026 before Judge Dorsey; a June 18, 2026 pretrial conference is on the Kroll-administered calendar for a separate adversary proceeding. Recovery counsel with exposure to Genesis Digital Assets claims should monitor those proceedings closely for any settlement-posture shift.

Phishing risk remains elevated. Phishing emails impersonating the FTX Recovery Trust are actively circulating; fake versions of the Customer Portal have appeared online; FTX will never ask creditors to connect a wallet; any message requesting wallet access is a scam.

### II. Power Block Coin, LLC — Bankr. D. Utah No. 24-23041, Judge Cathleen D. Parker — Active Docket, Liquidating Trust Posture

Power Block Coin, LLC (d/b/a SmartFi) remains the most active domestic crypto-insolvency matter tracked in this briefing outside the 2022 mega-cluster. The debtor filed its voluntary Chapter 11 petition on **June 20, 2024**, and the case is pending before the Honorable Cathleen D. Parker in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court, District of Utah, Salt Lake City division, as Case No. 24-23041 — confirmed by the "CDP" docket prefix appearing on Doc 502 filed May 7, 2026.

The case arc has been defined by the tension between the debtor's initial Subchapter V election and the creditors' committee's drive toward a full Chapter 11 liquidation plan. The debtor filed its Chapter 11 petition with less than three hours to spare before the Subchapter V debt limit dropped from $7,500,000 to $3,024,725, electing to proceed under Subchapter V as an entity with aggregate noncontingent liquidated debts below the threshold. That eligibility was immediately contested. The Celsius-related Objecting Creditors asserted over **$133 million in preference claims** against the Debtor in connection with the pending Celsius bankruptcy (Case No. 22-10964, Bankr. S.D.N.Y.).

The Official Committee of Unsecured Creditors — represented by **Greenberg Traurig, LLP** — subsequently filed a comprehensive liquidation plan. The plan addresses claims totaling over **$192 million**, including significant claims from Celsius Network LLC exceeding $133 million, and proposes the appointment of a disinterested Liquidating Trustee to manage assets. Filed January 30, 2025, the plan outlines a strategy to maximize value for creditors through the establishment of a Liquidating Trust (__[Chapter11Cases.com](https://chapter11cases.com/blogs/news/creditors-committee-proposes-liquidation-plan-for-utah-crypto-firm-power-block-coin)__).

The creditors' committee has also flagged insider transaction concerns squarely relevant to any avoidance-action pipeline. The committee's filing raises concerns about pre-bankruptcy management, citing unexplained losses of cryptocurrency assets and questioning several insider transactions, including a **$17.4 million loan to Solara Communities LLC** — an affiliate developing real property in Toquerville, Utah — with concerns about this loan's unsecured status and recent encumbrances placed on the underlying property. The previous Subchapter V trustee, Joli A. Lofstedt, has already had fees allowed — $8,925.00, allowed as a Chapter 11 Administrative Claim under 11 U.S.C. § 330 on a final basis. Recovery counsel holding claims in the estate — particularly Celsius Litigation Administrators with the dominant $133M+ exposure — should monitor plan confirmation briefing and any adversary proceedings targeting the Solara Communities loan as the case moves toward a liquidating trust activation.

### III. Celsius Network — Post-Plan Litigation Administration, the Tether Recovery, and the $344.4 Million Distribution

Celsius Network LLC (Bankr. S.D.N.Y., No. 22-10964) confirmed its plan of reorganization on November 9, 2023, and the enterprise has now entered a Litigation Administration phase managed by court-appointed Litigation Administrators, with White & Case LLP serving as lead counsel to the Litigation Oversight Committee.

The newest recovery is the Tether judgment: the estate won nearly **$300 million** from Tether in its avoidance litigation, recovering a fraction of the **$4.3 billion** the Litigation Administrators had sought but adding materially to the creditor pool (__[The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/374625/celsius-wins-300-million-tether-bankruptcy-case-fraction-4-3-billion-claim)__). That outcome sits atop the **US$344.4 million** distribution White & Case facilitated to eligible Celsius creditors in January 2026, generated through the Litigation Administrators' continued efforts to monetize litigation claims and illiquid assets. To date, more than **US$3 billion** of cash and cryptocurrency has been distributed to Celsius creditors; the Litigation Oversight Committee has yielded **US$160 million** in preferential-transfer recoveries through contested proceedings and mediations conducted globally (__[White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/news/press-release/white-case-helps-secure-distribution-us3444-million-creditors-celsiuss-plan)__).

On the personnel front, a March 2026 court agreement resolved one of the estate's residual insider exposure items: Alex Mashinsky forfeited all claims to Celsius bankruptcy proceeds, with AM Ventures Holdings Inc., Koala1 LLC, and Koala3 LLC also barred from receiving any distribution; the bankruptcy court retained jurisdiction to oversee enforcement. This outcome followed Mashinsky's December guilty plea to criminal charges. The forfeitures free up reserved amounts for pro-rata redistribution to other allowed creditors.

Earn account holder recovery rates have drifted upward from early-case projections. The largest creditor class — earn account holders — now expects 35% to 45% recovery; initial estimates projected 28% to 38%, but successful asset sales pushed the range higher. The estate's clawback posture continues: in February 2026, the estate won a clawback lawsuit against early withdrawers, with users who pulled funds in the 90 days before bankruptcy required to return approximately **$76 million**, which flows back into the distribution pool. The claim objection window closes in September 2026, making this summer the effective last window for creditors to contest classification or payout amounts.

### IV. BlockFi, Genesis, and the 2022-Cluster Residue — Stragglers and Tail Risk

BlockFi Inc. (Bankr. D.N.J., No. 22-19361) achieved an effective 100% recovery outcome for eligible customers and general unsecured creditors at petition-date dollar values following its sale of FTX/Alameda claims, which followed an $874.5 million in-principle settlement with the FTX and Alameda Research estates in March 2024. The residual risk pool resides with non-U.S. creditors. As of April 2025, 97% of U.S. customers had claimed their distributions, but only 43% of non-U.S. customers had received theirs — with a May 15, 2025 deadline then looming. Under the Bankruptcy Code, assets not claimed by the deadline are shared among other unsecured creditors lower in priority. Claims-recovery counsel advising non-U.S. constituents should assess any remaining cure procedures with plan administrator Mohsin Y. Meghji of M3 Partners.

Genesis Global remains relevant for its dollarization-cap resolution: the plan went effective in August 2024, when BTC creditors received 51% of their in-kind coin-for-coin value (166% of petition-date value), ETH creditors received 66% in-kind (153% of petition-date value), and US-dollar creditors received 100%. The Genesis LOC distribution vote on the $3.2 billion DCG claims plan is calendared for June 18 (__[Creditor Rights Coalition](https://creditorcoalition.org/how-genesis-solved-the-dollarization-cap-dilemma-for-crypto-creditors/)__).

The broader 2022-cluster precedent on cryptocurrency claim valuation — applying petition-date dollar figures under 11 U.S.C. § 502(b) rather than current market prices — continues to shape recovery expectations across all cases. The Voyager, Celsius, and BlockFi debtors confirmed plans without directly litigating the issue — a structural settlement of the crypto-valuation question that will bear on any future crypto insolvency filing in a rising-market environment.

## Recommended Actions

Creditors and recovery counsel across the tracked matters should treat the next four days as operationally decisive: the **FTX June 16, 2026 record date** is a hard bar — any creditor who has not completed KYC, tax documentation, and platform onboarding with BitGo, Kraken, or Payoneer will be locked out of the July 31 distribution, with no guarantee of a subsequent cure window, and transferred-claim holders must confirm their transfer is reflected on the official claims register before the 21-day objection clock expires. Counsel holding Power Block Coin (No. 24-23041, Judge Parker, Bankr. D. Utah) claims — particularly the dominant Celsius Litigation Administrator block with $133M+ in asserted preference exposure — should pull the latest docket entries from CourtListener and assess whether any reflects an adversary complaint, plan confirmation scheduling order, or asset-sale motion; the $17.4M Solara Communities loan and the committee's insider-transaction findings remain live avoidance-action targets. In the Celsius post-plan administration (No. 22-10964, Bankr. S.D.N.Y.), claimants who have not yet completed claims-portal onboarding should do so before the September 2026 final claim-objection window, and should note that the ~$300M Tether recovery augments the pool against which the revised 35%–45% earn-account range is modeled. Genesis claimants should calendar the June 18 LOC distribution vote on the $3.2B DCG plan. For BlockFi non-U.S. creditors who missed the 2025 deadline sweep, engage plan administrator M3 Partners directly regarding any subordination cure path under 11 U.S.C. § 726. Finally, all practitioners should be alert to the active phishing campaign targeting FTX and Celsius creditors — official communications will never request wallet connections or one-time passcodes outside the authenticated portal login flow.
