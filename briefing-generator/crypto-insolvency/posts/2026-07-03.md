Five buyers walked out of the Bitcoin Depot auction on June 29. One court date now controls everything: July 8, 9:00 a.m. CT, before Judge Lopez in S.D. Tex.

The kiosks, the BitAccess software stack, BDCheckout, and the Kutt P2P platform were carved across five winning bidders — a fragmented result that reflects both the breadth of the asset pool and the fractured demand for distressed BTM infrastructure. The macro context is not subtle: crypto ATM fraud hit $389 million in reported losses last year, a 58% jump from 2024, and state AGs in Massachusetts and Iowa had already sued the company before the petition date.

Meanwhile in Delaware, BlockFills crossed its own inflection point Tuesday at 4:00 p.m. ET — voting deadline and confirmation-objection deadline both expired. Confirmation hearing: July 13, 10:00 a.m. ET, before Judge Horan. The math remains uncomfortable: $3.25 million Keyrock purchase price against $145 million in general unsecured customer and trade obligations. The commingling admission from the first-day hearing has not been resolved.

• BlockFills — Bankr. D. Del., No. 26-10371 — confirmation July 13
• Bitcoin Depot — Bankr. S.D. Tex. — sale hearing July 8; general bar date July 21, 5:00 p.m. CT

Practical note for creditors: the Bitcoin Depot July 21 bar date is non-negotiable for any party expecting to participate in liquidating plan distributions. File before then or forfeit your place in the waterfall.
