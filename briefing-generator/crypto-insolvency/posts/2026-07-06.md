🗓 Seven days to confirmation. BlockFills / Reliz Technology Group Holdings (Bankr. D. Del., Case No. 26-10371) goes before Judge Horan on July 13 at 10:00 a.m. ET. The objection window closed July 1. That record is shut.

Where things stand:

• Sale — approved. Keyrock SA acquires substantially all assets for $3.25 million. Sale order cleared the June 16 hearing.
• Plan — Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan (Dkt. 391) goes to confirmation July 13.
• Unsecured pool — roughly $145 million in customer and trade obligations against a $3.25 million gross sale price. Recovery math runs almost entirely through post-confirmation litigation.

The controlling legal question — whether customer digital assets are estate property under §541 — was never resolved by adversary proceeding. Debtors' counsel confirmed at the first-day hearing that customer funds "have always been commingled with company funds." Debtors affirmatively treat all such funds as estate property. Creditors who did not file a Rule 7001(b) adversary proceeding to challenge that characterization have, on the debtors' argument, forfeited that fight.

If the plan confirms July 13, the commingling admission becomes the operative factual foundation for the distribution waterfall. Unsecured customer claimants recover pro rata, not on a segregated-asset basis.

Practical takeaway: If you hold a claim in this estate and no objection was filed on your behalf before July 1, shift focus now to claim-allowance mechanics under Dkt. 391 and monitor what litigation recovery vehicle, if any, survives confirmation.
