## LinkedIn

Three years after the crypto credit collapse, the recoveries are real — and the gating risk is now administrative, not legal.

Where the big estates stand:

• FTX: ~$10B returned to creditors cumulatively. ~$2.2B went out March 31; the next round is targeted for end of July. Preferred equity holders even reached a payout in May — extraordinary in a fraud case. The estate is litigating a $1B clawback against Genesis Digital Assets.
• Celsius: Entering what should be its final major distribution year under the Fahrenheit plan. Founder Alex Mashinsky is serving 12 years; victim restitution is still pending.
• Genesis: Distributing under the DCG settlement; in-kind BTC elections have outperformed cash.

The practical lesson for claimants: the money is moving on record dates. If your KYC isn't current or you miss a record date, you don't get paid that round even if your claim is allowed. Verification discipline is now the whole game.
