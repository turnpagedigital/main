MVMT Labs, Inc. — the original developer of the Movement blockchain — filed Chapter 11 on July 15 in Bankr. D. Del., Case No. 26-11113, assigned to Judge Horan. Subchapter V. Same judge as BlockFills next door.

The balance sheet tells the story: up to $10M in liabilities, as many as 299 creditors, and a largest unsecured claim of $1.6M held by former executive Rushi Manche. The collapse traces to the controversial market-maker distribution of 66M MOVE tokens — triggering exchange bans, steep price drops, and ultimately this filing. MOVE now trades at roughly $0.0104, down ~94%.

The structural question worth watching: Move Industries assumed operations from MVMT Labs in December 2025 — one month before the insolvency picture crystallized. Whether that transfer holds up under §548 fraudulent-transfer scrutiny, given the asset/liability mismatch and timing, is the threshold issue for creditor recovery here.
