🪙 $52M stalking-horse bid. $173.1M in liabilities. The math on Poolin Technology's Chapter 11 is brutal before anyone pays a lawyer.

Poolin Technology PTE. Ltd., Lonestar Dream Inc., and Lonestar Taproot LLC filed Ch. 11 in D.N.J. on July 22, 2026 (No. 26-18325). The debtors owe roughly $163.7M in wallet IOUs to approximately 11,700 users — accounts frozen since September 2022. Thor CALAP LLC's $52M stalking-horse for the Pyote and Tarbush, Texas mining sites covers ~31.8% of those IOUs gross, before fees, with only $1.2M cash on hand. Real recovery will be materially lower.

Two structural problems compound the arithmetic:

1. The $52M bid covers assets held by the Lonestar Texas affiliates, not by the Singapore wallet entity. The intercompany boundary between Poolin Technology and the Lonestar subsidiaries will drive the distribution question under any liquidating plan.

2. Claims are denominated in BTC, ETH, and USDT but must be valued in USD as of the July 22, 2026 petition date under § 502(b) — nearly four years after the 2022 freeze. Bitcoin's trajectory over that period likely inflates the dollar-denominated claim pool relative to distributable fiat proceeds.

The § 541 question — whether wallet deposits were held in trust or transferred title to the estate — remains unresolved. The Celsius court (Bankr. S.D.N.Y.) found unsecured creditor status on comparable TOS language. That holding does not bind D.N.J.

Practical step for IOU holders: file a request for special notice under FRBP 2002 now and watch for the U.S. Trustee's committee appointment and the § 363 bidding procedures motion setting the auction floor.
