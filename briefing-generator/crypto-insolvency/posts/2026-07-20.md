## LinkedIn

$3.25 million closed against $145 million owed.

Keyrock completed its acquisition of BlockFills' institutional trading and brokerage assets on July 16. The math is the story. General unsecured customer and trade claims total roughly $145 million; the sale generated $3.25 million. Celsius holds secured promissory notes of ~$4.8 million with first-priority liens. What remains for unsecured creditors runs through a liquidating trust pursuing insider liability, fraudulent-transfer claims, and claim reconciliation — not sale proceeds.

The commingling issue is settled against customers by the estate's own words. Debtors' counsel confirmed at the first-day hearing that customer funds were commingled with company and other customer funds, and that the estate treats them as property of the estate under § 541. No tracing argument survives that admission without independent evidence of segregation.

Practical takeaway: If you hold a BlockFills claim and received pre-petition representations that your assets were custodied or segregated, preserve that record now — emails, statements, marketing materials — before a confirmation order enters with broad discharge language.
