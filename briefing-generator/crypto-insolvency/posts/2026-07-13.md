⚖️ BlockFills confirmation hearing is on the docket right now — 10:00 a.m. ET, Judge Horan, Bankr. D. Del.

*In re Reliz Technology Group Holdings Inc., et al.*, No. 26-10371 (TMH), reached this point in roughly 120 days from petition. That's compressed. The § 363 sale to Brussels-based Keyrock SA at $3.25 million closed the asset-disposition question. What Judge Horan is reviewing this morning is whether the Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan (Dkt. No. 391) clears the § 1129(a) bar — best interests, feasibility, good faith — against a capital structure carrying approximately $145 million in general unsecured customer and trade claims.

The math is blunt: $3.25 million in sale proceeds against $145 million in GUC exposure. The GUC trust / NewCo toggle structure defers the real fight — Celsius's secured claim priority versus customer claimants — into distribution mechanics rather than resolving it through a contested § 541 ruling on whether customer crypto deposits were property of the estate.

The Ward Trust recoupment order (Dkt. No. 357) already locked in a $71,708.25/BTC petition-date price. That number carries downstream weight for how the broader GUC pool gets valued.

Claim objection deadline: September 11, 2026.

Practical takeaway: Pull the confirmation order once entered today. Read the release and exculpation provisions before assuming your claim treatment is final. The Celsius lien position and GUC waterfall are where recoveries will actually be determined.
