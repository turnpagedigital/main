⚖️ BlockFills confirmation hearing is tomorrow — Monday, July 13, 10:00 a.m. ET, Courtroom 5, Wilmington, Delaware before Judge Horan.

The core issue isn't the $3.25M Keyrock sale. It's whether the plan can treat commingled customer digital assets as estate property under § 541 and distribute pro rata — without ever running an adversary proceeding to adjudicate the question.

The facts on commingling are not in dispute. On February 11, 2026, Debtors' representatives disclosed to clients that assets were "not segregated per client" and were pooled into "one balance sheet." A counterparty transferred 40 BTC and 650,050 USDC on January 16; the wallet was empty within 1.5 hours.

The Debtors' response is procedural: objectors never filed an adversary proceeding under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7001(b), and no tracing was attempted. That argument may hold. If it does, $145M in unsecured claims recovers against a $3.25M sale price — deeply distressed math.

Fifteen docket entries landed in the 72 hours ending July 10. The objection window closed July 1. Parties who missed it have largely forfeited substantive grounds; preserved due-process arguments tied to the § 541 determination are the remaining lever.
