🪙 $2.8B debut. 26% first-day gain. Former Celsius claimholders finally have a liquid exit — but read the fine print before you celebrate.

Ionic Digital (IOND) completed its Nasdaq direct listing on July 28. The company was assembled from Celsius Network's mining assets, and 37 million Class A shares were distributed to eligible Celsius claimholders through the restructuring. Registered stockholders can now sell up to 10.8 million shares. The stock opened at $50 — below the $53 reference price set by the June 2026 Series A raise.

The business is no longer a miner. Ionic decommissioned Bitcoin mining at its 234 MW Ward County, Texas facility in December 2025 and leased the site to AI infrastructure provider Nscale under a 126-month triple-net agreement carrying ~$1.95B in contracted revenue. 2026 revenue is projected at $195M.

The listing does not close the Celsius avoidance docket. On July 13, a New York bankruptcy judge issued a first-of-its-kind damages framework governing cryptocurrency valuation in § 547 preference actions — a ruling White & Case says will govern nearly 1,000 pending cases. International customers who withdrew over $100,000 pre-collapse remain subject to U.S. clawback litigation.

• Celsius / IOND: Direct listing effective July 28 (Nasdaq: IOND). Avoidance docket active; July 13 damages framework now controls clawback valuation methodology.
• BlockFills (*In re Reliz*, D. Del. 26-10371): § 363 sale to Keyrock SA closed July 16 at $3.25M against ~$145M in general unsecured claims. Six new docket entries filed July 28–29 — pull PACER before assuming confirmation posture is settled.

**Practical takeaway:** If you received a § 547 demand notice in the Celsius avoidance docket, the July 13 ruling set the governing valuation methodology. That framework tightens settlement leverage. Engage counsel now — before defaults accumulate.
