## LinkedIn

$3.25M. That's the approved sale price for BlockFills — against a liability stack that includes ~$145M in general unsecured claims.

Judge Horan signed off on the Keyrock SA acquisition in *In re Reliz Technology Group Holdings Inc.*, Case No. 26-10371 (Bankr. D. Del.) on June 16. Keyrock, a Brussels-based market maker, assumes substantially all assets, select liabilities, and BlockFills' IP. Confirmation is targeted for July 13.

The math is stark. Reliz reported $50–100M in assets against $100–500M in liabilities. The $3.25M sale proceeds alone imply near-zero general unsecured recovery. What happens next depends almost entirely on adversary litigation — including Adv. Pro. 26-50230 against Artha Investment Partners, whose response deadline was this morning — and whether customer-property arguments under § 541 pull any assets outside the estate.

The UCC (appointed March 27) has FTI Consulting deployed. The docket remains active through the July 13 window.

The customer-property question — bailment/trust theory vs. estate property — has not been adjudicated. Plan confirmation will force a posture on it.

Practical takeaway: If you hold a claim in 26-10371, verify whether your liability was assumed by Keyrock under the APA or remains with the estate. Those are two different recovery paths. July 13 is close.
