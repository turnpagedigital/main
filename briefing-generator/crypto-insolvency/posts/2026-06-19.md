⚖️ $3.25M. That's what Keyrock SA is paying for BlockFills' operational core — approved Tuesday by Judge Horan in Bankr. D. Del. (No. 26-10371).

The § 363 sale closes the asset-transfer chapter. What it doesn't close: the § 541 question of whether customer digital assets are estate property or separately held. That dispute — the same framework litigated in Celsius (Bankr. S.D.N.Y., No. 22-10964) — runs straight through the plan confirmation process now ahead.

Case status, *In re Reliz Technology Group Holdings Inc., et al.*:
• Filed: March 15, 2026 — $50M–$100M assets vs. $100M–$500M liabilities
• ~$145M in general unsecured obligations
• Buyer: Keyrock SA (Brussels); assumes substantially all assets, IP, customer lists
• Next hard date: September 11, 2026 (4:00 p.m. ET) — Veritaglobal claims portal
• Prepetition actions (Dominion Capital, S.D.N.Y.; Alberta Ltd./Bertram, N.D. Ill.) stayed under § 362

Separately: the FTX Recovery Trust dismissed — with prejudice — its $1.15B clawback suit against Genesis Digital Assets (Bankr. D. Del., No. 1:25-ap-52358). No settlement disclosed. No rationale stated. That $1.15B is not coming back to the estate.

Practical note for claimants: Pull the BlockFills scheduling order from PACER or veritaglobal.net/BlockFills now. Confirm what September 11 triggers — confirmation hearing, plan supplement, or claims objection deadline. The answer changes your timeline.
