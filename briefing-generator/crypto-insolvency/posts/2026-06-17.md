$3.25M sale approved. $145M in unsecured claims. That ratio tells you everything about where BlockFills creditors stand.

Judge Horan (Bankr. D. Del.) signed off on the § 363 sale of substantially all Reliz Technology Group Holdings assets to Brussels-based Keyrock SA on June 16. Next hard date: plan confirmation targeted July 13, 2026.

The capital structure context is stark:
• ~$145M general unsecured customer and trade obligations
• ~$4.8M outstanding under Celsius secured promissory notes (first-priority lien)
• $3.25M purchase price — a roughly 44:1 gap against the GUC pool

Sub-5 cent recoveries on unsecured claims are the baseline unless estate-side avoidance actions move the number. The Dominion Capital complaint — which alleged BlockFills admitted to commingling customer assets and a balance sheet shortfall — is the most visible predicate for adversary proceedings under §§ 548–550. Watch Dkt. 431 and 433 (both June 16) for the sale order's asset-exclusion schedule. Assets not transferred to Keyrock are what the creditor pool actually has to work with before confirmation.

Regulatory approvals remain the residual closing condition on Keyrock's end.

Claims administrator: Veritaglobal (KCC dba Verita), (866) 554-5810.

Practical takeaway: If you hold BlockFills GUC claims, get the sale order's exclusion schedule now — residual assets and any fraudulent-transfer recoveries are your recovery story, not the $3.25M headline.
