BlockFills confirmation hearing ran this morning — July 13, 2026, 10:00 a.m. ET, Bankr. D. Del., Case No. 26-10371 (TMH) before Judge Horan. Whether he entered a confirmation order, required further briefing, or continued the hearing is not yet reflected in the public docket as of this morning's pull.

That outcome is the single most consequential open question in this case. Here's why it matters:

• Keyrock SA acquired substantially all assets for $3.25 million — roughly 2.2 cents on the dollar against ~$145 million in unsecured customer and trade claims.
• The entire remaining recovery thesis for customer-claimants turns on one question: are customer digital assets property of the estate under § 541, or held outside it in trust or bailment?
• Debtors' counsel admitted at the first day hearing that customer funds were commingled with company funds. The debtors' position is that all such funds are estate property.
• At least one creditor stated the onboarding agreement expressly promised segregation. That gap is the live dispute.
• If confirmation was entered July 13, the window to challenge the estate-property treatment is hardening now. Creditors who did not file a Bankruptcy Rule 7001(b) adversary proceeding before confirmation may have forfeited a standalone trust or bailment claim.

Monitor CourtListener docket ID 72494188 for the confirmation order. If you hold a customer claim and have not yet consulted restructuring counsel on adversary proceeding standing, that conversation is overdue.
