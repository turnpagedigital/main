BlockFills confirmation is Monday, July 13, 10:00 a.m. ET — Judge Horan, Bankr. D. Del., Case No. 26-10371 (TMH). The $3.25M Keyrock sale closed June 16. Four days later, the plan goes to confirmation.

The unresolved issue heading into that hearing: whether commingled customer digital assets are property of the estate under § 541. Debtors' counsel confirmed at the first-day hearing that customer funds "have always been commingled with company funds and other customer funds." No adversary proceeding under FRBP 7001(b) has been filed. No tracing attempt is on the record. That procedural gap likely forecloses any in-kind recovery argument at confirmation, leaving institutional depositors — roughly $145M in general unsecured obligations stacked against a $3.25M sale price — in a pro-rata liquidating trust alongside trade creditors.

The opt-out release dispute was deferred from disclosure statement approval and lands on July 13 as well. Judge Horan's ruling on that mechanism will determine how broadly non-debtor parties, including directors and officers, are shielded from customer claims post-confirmation.
