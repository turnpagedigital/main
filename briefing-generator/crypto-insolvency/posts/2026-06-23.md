$3.25M. That's what Keyrock SA is paying for "substantially all" assets of BlockFills — against a $145M general unsecured creditor pool.

The sale hearing ran June 16 before Judge Horan (Bankr. D. Del., 26-10371). No approved order has posted to the Verita docket as of yesterday's fetch. Status remains unconfirmed.

What matters more right now: plan solicitation is live. Docket No. 388 (May 28) launched the formal §1125 period. Confirmation is targeted for September 11, 2026.

Case posture at a glance:
• Assets: $50M–$100M reported. Liabilities: $100M–$500M reported. GUC pool: ~$145M.
• UCC members: SBI VC Trade, Dominion Capital, Energy Conversion Group, Fuel Labs, three individuals. FTI Consulting (financial advisor) + Morris James LLP (Delaware counsel) retained.
• §541 customer-property question unresolved — whether institutional deposits are estate property or held in trust is the central confirmation fight.
• Two pre-petition fraud/misappropriation suits (S.D.N.Y. and N.D. Ill.) stayed under §362; the underlying record feeds the UCC's insider-transfer investigation.
• Special Committee formed March 12 to investigate conflict matters.

The liquidating trust's recoverable litigation claims — not the Keyrock proceeds — are where GUC recovery lives or dies.

Practical takeaway: institutional counterparties who believe their deposits were held in a custodial or trust arrangement should engage restructuring counsel now, before the September 11 confirmation objection deadline. Verify solicitation materials at veritaglobal.net/blockfills.
