The structural fault line: debtors' counsel admitted at the first-day hearing that customer funds were commingled with company funds. Debtors' position is that all funds are estate property under § 541 — the same framing that drove the most contested phase of Celsius. The UCC has every incentive to challenge that characterization before confirmation.

Practical takeaway: If you hold a BlockFills customer claim, review your account agreement now. TOS language and commingling facts were outcome-determinative in Celsius. Don't concede the estate-property framing in plan negotiations without counsel reviewing your specific custodial terms.
