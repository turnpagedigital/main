• BlockFills / Reliz Technology — Bankr. D. Del., Case No. 26-10371, Judge Horan. Disclosure statement approved May 28. Confirmation hearing: July 13, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. ET. The plan's NewCo/§ 363 toggle is now operative — debtors must elect a distribution path before or at confirmation.

The customer-property question remains unresolved. Debtors' counsel confirmed at the first-day hearing that customer funds were commingled with company funds across one balance sheet. Under § 541, commingled assets are estate property unless a claimant can establish a trust or bailment through an adversary proceeding under Fed. R. Bankr. P. 7001(b). No such proceeding has been filed by objecting creditors. The Celsius outcome is the default precedent here.

Practical takeaway: Any claimant asserting a property or trust interest in specific digital assets has a hard deadline — the July 13 confirmation hearing. After that, plan releases and res judicata significantly narrow the path.
