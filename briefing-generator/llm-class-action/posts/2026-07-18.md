## LinkedIn

⚖️ $1.5B settlement. 440,490 works claimed. Still no final approval order.

Judge Martínez-Olguín took *Bartz v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal. 3:24-cv-05417) under submission after the May 14 fairness hearing. The last supplemental filing landed July 13 (DE 679). The court has not ruled.

That delay matters structurally. Anthropic's $450M Schedule Payment 1 is due September 25, 2026 — but checks only go out after final approval is final and any objector appeals are resolved. One Ninth Circuit appeal, even a weak one, pushes the October distribution window.

Two numbers define the claims picture:
• 91.3% participation rate (vs. 54% projected at the March 19 final approval motion)
• ~$3,100 per work, confirmed by class counsel Justin Nelson

On the broader docket, the sharper near-term date is October 21, 2026 — the cross-MSJ hearing in *Concord Music Group v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal. 5:24-cv-03811, Judge Lee). Universal, Concord, and ABKCO are asking the court to rule that training on commercially licensed lyrics is not fair use under § 107. A ruling against Anthropic there would be materially adverse for every LLM developer running unlicensed commercial training sets.

One thing Bartz does not resolve: Judge Alsup ruled training on legally purchased books is fair use. Bartz settles the piracy-dataset liability (LibGen/PiLiMi). The training-use question moves to other cases.

Practical note: if you have a Bartz claim, watch for the final approval order — that filing date, not the hearing date, starts the appellate clock that controls your distribution timing. Consult counsel on your specific position.
