$1.5B sitting in limbo. As of July 1, 2026, Judge Martínez-Olguín has not issued a final approval order in *Bartz v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal. No. 3:24-cv-05417). The CourtListener docket last moved June 25 — no new entries.

The hold isn't about the deal's substance. At the May 14 fairness hearing, the judge's questions targeted attorneys' fees (class counsel seeks $375M — 25% of the fund) and the cost-reserve structure. The merits of the settlement itself were not in dispute. Supplemental briefs from both sides are on the record. The court can rule without another hearing.

What's at stake while the clock runs:
• 440,490 of 482,460 eligible works claimed — 91.3% participation, far above any class-action norm
• Per-work payout holding near $3,100
• Anthropic's next installment ($375M tranche) due September 25, 2026 — likely before any Ninth Circuit appeal resolves if an objector moves
• Payouts to class members require full approval plus exhaustion of appeals

Separate but linked: in *Concord Music Group v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal. No. 5:24-cv-03811), cross-motions for summary judgment on fair use closed June 8. Judge Lee can rule at any time. That ruling — not Bartz — will be the first federal merits decision on whether AI training on licensed content is fair use under § 107.

Practical note for claimants: your payout clock doesn't start at settlement approval — it starts when appeals are exhausted. Build that lag into any expectations.
