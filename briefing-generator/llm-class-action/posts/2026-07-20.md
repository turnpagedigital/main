• *In re OpenAI*, No. 1:25-md-03143 (S.D.N.Y.) — The NYT-led plaintiff group filed a sanctions motion July 9 against OpenAI, alleging it told the court it lacked tools to search its systems, while an employee later testified the company had run multiple internal searches and maintained a 78-million-conversation database tracking infringement. Relief sought includes adverse evidentiary findings on ChatGPT regurgitation — which would gut OpenAI's § 107(4) market-harm defense on the news-publisher track.

• *Concord Music Group v. Anthropic*, No. 5:24-cv-03811 (N.D. Cal.) — Cross-motions for summary judgment fully briefed. Hearing set October 21, 2026 before Judge Eumi K. Lee. First scheduled federal ruling on AI training and music-lyric fair use.
