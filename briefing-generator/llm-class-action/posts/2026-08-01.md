## LinkedIn

$1.5 billion. Final judgment entered July 20, 2026. *Bartz v. Anthropic*, No. 3:24-cv-05417 (N.D. Cal.).

Judge Martínez-Olguín approved the settlement in a 23-page order, overruled all 53 objections, and confirmed the per-work recovery at roughly $3,000 — four times the § 504(c)(2) statutory minimum for willful infringement. Claims rate: 92.77% by works. The fee award came in at ~$101.5 million, well below the $187.5 million counsel requested.

Three things the order preserved that matter going forward:

• Output-side claims are not released. The settlement covers only input-side conduct through August 25, 2025.
• Non-class members received an express judicial reminder that they remain free to bring their own claims.
• Anthropic's representation that neither LibGen nor PiLiMi appeared in any commercially released model is now embedded in the final judgment — any future contradictory disclosure carries direct exposure.

Next fund installment: $375 million on September 25, 2026 — 55 days out. Distribution has not commenced. JND Legal Administration holds operational control; the settlement portal is anthropiccopyrightsettlement.com.

Meanwhile, *Concord Music Group v. Anthropic*, No. 5:24-cv-03811, filed a Second Amended Complaint on July 22 incorporating discovery-derived evidence of internal employee prompting for copyrighted lyrics and alleged CMI removal under 17 U.S.C. § 1202(b). Partial summary judgment on liability remains pending before Judge Lee.

Practical takeaway: If your works are on the 482,460-work Works List and you have not confirmed your claim status with JND, do that now before distribution mechanics are set.
