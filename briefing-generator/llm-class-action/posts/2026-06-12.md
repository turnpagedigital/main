Thomson Reuters v. ROSS (3d Cir., No. 25-2153): the first appellate fair-use ruling in an AI-training case argued Wednesday. The panel spent its energy on factor four — market harm — and ROSS's direct rivalry with Westlaw. If the court affirms on a commercial-substitution theory, that reasoning travels straight into the generative-AI docket, where general-purpose LLMs compete in every information market at once. Bartz v. Anthropic (N.D. Cal., No. 3:24-cv-05417): the $1.5B settlement's June 11 distribution-calculation date has passed, but Judge Martínez-Olguín still hasn't entered final approval. Until she does, the 30-day appeal clock doesn't start and JND can't pay. 91.3% of eligible works are claimed; the per-work figure is $3,100.
