## LinkedIn

⚖️ $1.5B and still waiting: *Bartz v. Anthropic* final approval has not issued as of the June 25 docket sync.

Judge Martínez-Olguín held the May 14 fairness hearing, asked pointed questions about the $375M fee request, and took the matter under submission. No order yet. The settlement administrator's distribution calculation — originally projected for June 11 — is rolling forward day for day until that order lands.

Key figures on the record:

• 440,490 of 482,460 eligible works claimed (91.3% rate)
• Per-work payout confirmed at ~$3,100 — the high claims rate collapsed earlier projections of a larger figure
• Net-of-fees fund at the full 25%: ~$843M, or roughly $1,748 per claimed work before admin costs
• Anthropic funds in four installments; next tranche due September 25, 2026

One structural point that gets missed: *Bartz* settles only piracy-acquisition claims — works Anthropic copied from LibGen and the Pirate Library Mirror. Judge Alsup ruled in June 2025 that legally-acquired training data is fair use; he denied that protection for pirated sources. The settlement resolves the latter. It creates no precedent on AI training broadly.

Meanwhile, *Concord Music Group v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal.) completed cross-MSJ briefing June 8. Judge Lee's ruling will be the first federal merits decision on § 107 fair use for commercial AI training on licensed music. That one moves markets.

Practical takeaway: If you filed a *Bartz* claim, watch CourtListener docket 69058235 for the final approval order — that date, not June 11, is now the clock that governs your distribution timeline.
