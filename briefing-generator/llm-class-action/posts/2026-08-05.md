$1.5 billion, 92.77% claims rate, no payout date yet — that's where *Bartz v. Anthropic* stands this morning.

Judge Martínez-Olguín's July 20 final-approval order is two weeks old. The settlement administration phase is now the live story. A few hard facts worth keeping in view:

• Per-work payment: ~$3,000 — four times the statutory minimum for willful infringement under 17 U.S.C. § 504(c)
• Class: 482,460 works on the Works List, each carrying an ISBN/ASIN and a U.S. Copyright Office registration within five years of first publication
• Scope of release: past conduct only, through August 25, 2025 — future-conduct and AI-output claims are explicitly carved out
• Anthropic must destroy all original LibGen/PiLiMi files and any derived copies
• Pearson PLC has disclosed it is a claimant but has not recorded any recovery in its interim financials, citing uncertainty on amount and timing
• No payout commencement notice has issued as of this morning; monitor AnthropicCopyrightSettlement.com

Meanwhile, the next hard milestone on this desk is October 21, 2026 — summary judgment hearing in *Concord Music Group v. Anthropic*, N.D. Cal., before Judge Lee. That ruling on whether lyric ingestion is fair use will set the doctrinal frame for music-side AI training claims.

Practical note for claimants: if you filed a claim in *Bartz*, watch the settlement administrator portal for payout timing. No distribution schedule has been announced.
