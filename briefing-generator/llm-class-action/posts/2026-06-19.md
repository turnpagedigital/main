$1.5B on the table — but no final-approval order has been entered.

Judge Martínez-Olguín took *Bartz v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal. No. 3:24-cv-05417) under submission after the May 14 fairness hearing. As of the last docket sync (June 4, entry 674), no written order exists. That matters practically: the settlement administrator's June 11 distribution-calculation milestone and any summer disbursement are functionally suspended until the order drops.

Key figures on the record:
• 440,490 of 482,460 eligible works claimed — 91.3% participation rate
• ~$3,100 projected per-work payout
• Attorneys' fees sought: $375M (25% of fund)
• Court questioned fees, service awards, and cost reserve at hearing — no rubber stamp signaled

Meanwhile, *Concord Music Group v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal. No. 5:24-cv-03811) is the next live pressure point. Cross-motions for summary judgment on fair use are fully briefed before Judge Lee. The output-reproduction theory — Claude generating copyrighted lyrics verbatim — was never adjudicated in *Bartz*, which settled on piracy-acquisition grounds only. Judge Alsup's June 2025 training-data fair-use ruling does not reach it.

A Concord I ruling before fall would be the first federal court decision directly adjudicating whether AI training on licensed lyrics is fair use under § 107.

Practical takeaway: If you have a *Bartz* claim, pull PACER entries 675 onward on No. 3:24-cv-05417 before treating any disbursement timeline as firm. Even one objector pursuing a Ninth Circuit appeal delays initial distribution by twelve to eighteen months.
