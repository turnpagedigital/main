## LinkedIn

📋 $3,100. That is the locked per-work payout in *Bartz v. Anthropic* — and no final approval order has issued yet.

Judge Martínez-Olguín took the matter under submission after the May 14 fairness hearing. Two new docket entries appeared June 25, but their contents are not publicly available through RECAP. The order remains pending.

What the record shows:
• 440,490 of 482,460 eligible works claimed — 91.3% participation, far above the typical ~10% class action rate
• Attorneys' fees revised down to $187.5M (12.5% of fund) from the original $375M ask
• Anthropic funds the settlement in four installments; the next tranche — $450M — is due September 25, 2026
• No distribution until final approval issues and any objector appeal window closes (30 days to the Ninth Circuit)

Seven objectors presented at the hearing. If any notice an appeal after the final order issues, distribution could slip into 2027 regardless of when the order lands.

One framing point that matters: *Bartz* is a piracy remedy — Anthropic's torrenting of LibGen and Pirate Library Mirror — not a ruling on AI training fair use. The surrounding litigation (Concord II's $3.1B torrenting claim, the OpenAI MDL's 108M-log discovery posture) remains entirely live.

Practical takeaway: Any claim-trading thesis that assumes near-term distribution must price in both the appeal window and the installment schedule. The math does not support full-fund distribution before September 25, 2026 at the earliest.
