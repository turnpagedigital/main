$1.5B. That's the final number in *Bartz v. Anthropic* — and as of July 20, it's done.

Judge Martínez-Olguín entered final approval of the class action settlement on July 20, 2026. A few figures worth having in your files:

• **Per-work recovery:** ~$3,100+ — four times the statutory minimum for willful infringement, and an order of magnitude above the maximum proposed in the rejected 2011 *Google Books* settlement
• **Claims rate:** 91.3% — 440,490 of 482,460 eligible works claimed; the court called it "overwhelmingly favorable"
• **Fee award:** ~$101.5M approved on a 3.75× lodestar multiplier — down from the $187.5M counsel requested; 10% withheld pending post-distribution accounting
• **Opt-outs:** 350 valid opt-outs covering 1,802 works (~0.03% of eligible works)
• **Non-monetary relief:** Anthropic must destroy all files torrented from Library Genesis and Pirate Library Mirror; settlement preserves future claims

The case has now crossed from litigation into claims administration. No distribution date has been announced. The Ninth Circuit appeal window runs 30 days from July 20 — it closes **August 19, 2026**.

**Practical note for rights holders:** If you opted out, the court confirmed you retain independent claims. Watch the August 19 window; any appeal filed then reshapes the distribution timeline.
