• ~$3,100 projected per-work payout (modestly improved after attorney fees were cut from $375M to $187.5M)
• Anthropic must also destroy all files torrented from LibGen and PiLiMi

Distribution clock is now running. The next Anthropic funding tranche is September 25, 2026 ($450M). Class covers copyright owners of books downloaded from LibGen (June 2021) or PiLiMi (July 2022) bearing an ISBN or ASIN, with Copyright Office registration within five years of first publication.
