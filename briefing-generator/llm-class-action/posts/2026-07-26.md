$1.5 billion — that number is now locked. Judge Martínez-Olguín signed the final judgment in *Bartz v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal. No. 3:24-cv-05417) on July 20, 2026. The case is out of litigation entirely. Distribution is the only question left.

The numbers from the approval order:
• 92.77% claims rate — 440,490 works claimed out of the class universe
• 350 valid opt-outs covering 1,802 works (.03% of total)
• ~$3,100 per author across 480,000+ works
• Fees: court approved ~$101.5M against a trimmed ask of $187.5M and an original ask of $300M — the lodestar cross-check drove that reduction materially
• 53 objections filed; all overruled
• Non-class-member objectors were reminded they remain free to bring independent claims

Distribution has no scheduled date. JND Legal Administration (Anthropic Copyright Settlement Administrator) controls the clock. The Authors Guild expects it to move quickly. Any disputed claims should enter the mutual-agreement process with the administrator now — Special Master submissions go under seal and the queue will fill.

One structural note for practitioners: the class definition embeds the 17 U.S.C. § 411 registration prerequisite directly. Works that cleared that threshold and didn't opt out are inside the fund. Works outside the LibGen/PiLiMi sourcing parameters are outside it — and the June 2025 fair-use ruling (pirated-source training ≠ fair use) travels forward into every parallel matter.

Practical takeaway: if you hold a disputed claim, initiate the administrator resolution process before the Special Master queue fills. Waiting costs position.
