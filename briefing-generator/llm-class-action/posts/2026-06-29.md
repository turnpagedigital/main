## LinkedIn

⚖️ $1.5 billion — and the final approval order still isn't signed.

The *Bartz v. Anthropic* fairness hearing ran 75 minutes on May 14. Judge Martínez-Olguín didn't approve that day, but the tone led most observers to expect approval to follow quickly. Distribution calculations were scheduled for June 11. We are now 18 days past that milestone with no confirmed final approval order in the public record.

What's holding the clock: the fee request. Class counsel is seeking $375 million — 25% of the fund. The judge asked for three supporting cases at the hearing. That question is still under submission.

What's not in question: the per-work payout (~$3,000–$3,100) and a 92.77% opt-in rate. Objector and opt-out rates were described as "minuscule."

One scope point that keeps getting lost: *Bartz* is not a training-data settlement. Judge Alsup's June 2025 fair-use ruling — that training on lawfully acquired books is permissible — stands untouched. This settlement covers Anthropic's alleged downloading from LibGen and PiLiMi. Those are different claims.

Payment mechanics matter here. Anthropic pays in tranches: an initial amount due within five business days of final approval, $450M on September 25, 2026, and another $450M on September 25, 2027. Any objector Ninth Circuit appeal extends the timeline six to twelve months.

Practical note for claimants: verify order status on CourtListener docket 69058235 before communicating any payment date. The clock doesn't start until the order issues.
