## LinkedIn

$101.5M in fees. $1.378B left for the class. That math matters.

Judge Martínez-Olguín entered final judgment in *Bartz v. Anthropic* (N.D. Cal.) on July 20, cutting class counsel's fee request from $187.5M to $101.5M — roughly 8.1% of the $1.5B fund — after a lodestar cross-check. Total costs land at $122M, well below the 25% benchmark counsel argued for. The difference flows directly to the per-work recovery pool.

Key figures from the order:
• 440,490 works covered, ~$3,100 per author
• 92.77% claims rate — highest on record for a copyright class action
• 350 opt-outs representing 1,802 works (0.03% of total)
• 53 objections filed; all overruled

The release is input-only, through August 25, 2025. Output claims survive. Future conduct claims survive. The 350 opt-outs and plaintiffs like Carreyrou and Chicken Soup for the Soul remain free to litigate independently.

Distribution timing: the 30-day appeal window runs from July 20, so no checks before August 19 at the earliest. The next installment — $450M — is scheduled September 25, 2026.

Meanwhile, the Third Circuit's ruling in *Thomson Reuters v. Ross Intelligence* (No. 25-2153) remains under submission after June 11 oral argument. That decision on AI training fair use will land before year-end and directly shape the *Concord Music* MSJ set for October 21.

Practical note: opt-out claimants building output or future-conduct cases should be assembling § 107(4) licensing-market comparator evidence now, before the Third Circuit ruling sets the transformativeness floor.
