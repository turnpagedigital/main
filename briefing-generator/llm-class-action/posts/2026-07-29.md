## LinkedIn

$101.5M in attorneys' fees — not the $187.5M requested, and far below the original $300M ask. That gap stays in the fund for authors.

On July 20, Judge Martínez-Olguín (N.D. Cal.) entered final judgment in *Bartz v. Anthropic*, No. 3:24-cv-05417, approving the $1.5 billion class action settlement. The fee ruling is the most consequential near-term variable for per-work recovery: every dollar trimmed from counsel's ask improves net distribution.

Key figures from the order:
• 440,490 of 482,460 eligible works claimed — 91.3% participation rate, roughly nine times the typical class action benchmark
• Named plaintiff service awards cut from $50,000 to $15,000 each
• All 53 objections overruled

The payment waterfall runs: $300M due within 30 days of the July 20 order; $450M on September 25, 2026; $450M on September 25, 2027. Payments to claimants are unlikely before late fall 2026 at the earliest.

Two dates to watch: August 19, 2026 is the Ninth Circuit appeal deadline for any of the 53 overruled objectors. A filed appeal delays the first distribution run. The fund is non-reversionary — unclaimed residual flows to the class, not back to Anthropic.

Takeaway: If you submitted a timely claim, confirm your work appears in the AnthropicCopyrightSettlement.com database now. Special Master disputes remain open for administratively contested claims, but the court's order makes those decisions final with no further judicial review.
