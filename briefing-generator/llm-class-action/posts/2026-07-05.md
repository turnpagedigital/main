## LinkedIn

$1.5B, no final order yet. The Bartz v. Anthropic fairness hearing closed May 14 before Judge Martínez-Olguín in N.D. Cal. She took it under submission. As of the July 2 CourtListener sync, the final approval order has not been filed. Distribution calculations are running, but payouts cannot issue until approval is entered and any appeals resolve.

A few numbers worth holding:

• 440,490 of 482,460 eligible works claimed — a 92.77% claim rate that is structurally anomalous for a class action
• Per-work payout: ~$3,100
• Attorneys' fees revised from $375M to $187.5M at hearing
• First $300M already in escrow; remaining tranches due Sept. 25, 2026 and Sept. 25, 2027

One thing the settlement does NOT resolve: Anthropic's right to train on lawfully acquired books. Judge Alsup's June 2025 fair-use ruling on that question stands. What the $1.5B covers is liability for downloading works from LibGen and PiLiMi — piracy-based infringement under § 504, not training-use infringement under § 107.

The § 107 question is very much alive elsewhere. Concord I (No. 5:24-cv-03811) is fully briefed for summary judgment before Judge Lee — the first federal merits ruling on AI training and song lyrics. The Third Circuit heard argument June 11 in Thomson Reuters v. Ross on the same § 107(4) market-harm issue.

Practical takeaway for claimants: check PACER (3:24-cv-05417) for the final approval order. Once entered, objector-appellants have 60 days under FRAP 4(a)(1)(B). An appellate stay could hold the unfunded tranches indefinitely.
