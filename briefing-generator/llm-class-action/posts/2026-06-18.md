## LinkedIn

⚖️ 91.3% claims rate in *Bartz v. Anthropic* is the number reshaping every AI copyright settlement model right now.

440,490 of 482,460 eligible works were claimed — nearly 10x the typical class action rate. That surge compresses per-work payouts toward the $3,100 figure class counsel cited at the May 14 fairness hearing, down from earlier projections that assumed far fewer claimants would file.

After fees ($187.5M, already cut from $300M following judicial pushback), administration costs, service awards, and Special Master fees, net recovery per work is likely in the $2,600–$2,800 range. Model from there, not the headline number.

Where things stand today:
• *Bartz v. Anthropic*, No. 3:24-cv-05417 (N.D. Cal.) — final approval order still not entered as of June 12 docket sync; June 11 distribution-calculation milestone has likely slipped
• Payment to claimants cannot begin until final approval enters and any appeals resolve; Anthropic funds via installments through September 2027
• Late opt-out question unresolved; objector appeals could push first payments into 2027

One thing the settlement does not resolve: the fair use question on AI training itself. Judge Alsup ruled training on legally acquired corpora is fair use — but that holding covers only the three named plaintiffs, not the certified class. Broader training liability is still open across the docket.

Practical takeaway: if you're tracking a Bartz claim, watch CourtListener docket 69058235 for the final approval order and any Ninth Circuit appeal filings before acting on distribution timing assumptions.
