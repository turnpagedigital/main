## LinkedIn

📋 Docket Entry 679 is in. Anthropic has filed its opposition to objectors in *Bartz v. Anthropic*, No. 3:24-cv-05417 (N.D. Cal.) — the final substantive brief the court authorized. Judge Martínez-Olguín held the Rule 23(e) fairness hearing May 14 and took the matter under submission. No ruling yet as of July 15.

Three mechanics claimants should track now:

• **Claims volume changed the math.** The claims rate hit 92.77%, with 99,000+ claims covering 273,331 works. Earlier forecasts of a premium payout built on a 54% claims rate are off the table. Per-work recovery likely lands near the original ~$3,000 figure.

• **Fee award is still open.** Class counsel revised their request down from $300M to $187.5M (20% → 12.5% of the $1.5B fund) after bench skepticism at the May 14 hearing. Any further reduction by the judge directly enlarges the net distributable fund.

• **First $300M is already in escrow.** Anthropic has funded that tranche in an interest-bearing account. Distribution clock starts on final approval — and resolution of any appeals.

One structural issue that doesn't resolve on final approval: Judge Alsup's class definition covers both legal and beneficial copyright owners. Where a publisher holds the exclusive license and an author holds the beneficial interest, a $3,000 per-work payment may require a separate royalty-allocation dispute to determine who captures what share.

Practical takeaway: Before the final approval order issues, any holder of an author-side claim should confirm whether a publisher holds the exclusive license on the specific works at issue.
