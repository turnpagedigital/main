⚖️ 52 days since the Bartz v. Anthropic fairness hearing. Still no written final approval order.

Judge Martínez-Olguín took the matter under submission on May 14. As of today, the CourtListener docket (last updated July 2) shows nothing. The court remains under submission.

What's locked in while we wait:

• 440,490 of 482,460 eligible works claimed — 91.3% claim rate against a typical class action rate near 10%
• ~$3,100 per-work payout anticipated
• $1.5B fund paid by Anthropic in installments: Oct 2025, Apr 2026, Sep 25 2026, Sep 25 2027
• Authors Guild estimates earliest payments in late fall — only after final approval AND any appeals are resolved

One thing the settlement does not do: it does not give Anthropic any forward-looking license to use class members' works. Rights-holders who filed claims retain full exclusive rights under 17 U.S.C. § 106 for future AI training negotiations. That distinction matters.

The September 25, 2026 installment is the near-term distribution pressure point. Any timely objector who perfects a Ninth Circuit appeal under Fed. R. App. P. 4(a)(1)(B) can delay that distribution wave — no supersedeas bond has been ordered.

Practical takeaway: Watch the N.D. Cal. docket daily. The 30-day appeal window opens the moment the order drops. Claimants who want the September installment cycle to hold should be ready to monitor for objector appeals and act.
