⚖️ $701M. That's what Sleep Country Canada paid for Sleep Number's assets — $286.8M more than its own stalking-horse bid of $415M.

Judge Kyu Young Paek (Bankr. S.D.N.Y., 26-11399) approved the § 363 sale to SNBR Inc. on July 20. Closing is targeted by July 31.

The capital stack math matters for unsecured creditors:
• $672.5M in secured debt
• $701M sale price ($529.5M cash + assumed obligations)
• $642.3M in assets vs. $1.3B in liabilities at filing

The gap between the secured debt load and the winning bid is thin — but the filing indicates funds will be available for unsecured distribution. How much reaches unsecured creditors depends on cure costs, professional fees, and resolution of remaining administrative claims. That math isn't settled yet.

The stalking-horse structure is worth noting as a process signal. A 38-day § 363 runway, a strategically motivated acquirer, and real auction competition produced a 69% premium over the floor. Brooklyn Bedding was the backup bidder. The DIP lender and the acquirer's parent were economically aligned — which reduced obstruction risk but creates its own structural tension for the unsecured class.

**Practical takeaway:** If you hold unsecured claims in 26-11399 and haven't filed a proof of claim, do it now. Monitor Kroll's restructuring site for the sale order, cure schedule, and any post-closing administrative bar date. Contact Davis Polk & Wardwell (Brian M. Resnick) for claims inquiries.
