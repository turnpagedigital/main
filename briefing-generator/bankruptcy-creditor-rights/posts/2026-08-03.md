$102M cash. $1.5B due August 1. The math didn't work.

Hughes Satellite Systems Corporation filed voluntary Chapter 11 in Bankr. S.D. Tex. (Houston Div.) today, August 3, 2026 — the second EchoStar-family petition in five weeks, following DISH DBS's prepackaged filing on June 30.

• Case: Hughes Satellite Systems Corporation et al., S.D. Tex. Houston Div. (docket pending on PACER)
• Debtor group: 11 subsidiaries including Hughes Network Systems, LLC and EchoStar Orbital L.L.C.
• Debt trigger: 5.25% Senior Secured Notes and 6.625% Senior Notes, both due 2026 — now automatically accelerated
• Posture: Free-fall. No DIP commitment, no RSA, no stalking-horse disclosed in first-day papers
• CRO: Robert Del Genio, FTI Consulting (Paul Gaske resigned all positions July 28)

Four items distressed holders should track now:

1. DIP terms — no commitment disclosed for a $1.5B+ filing is unusual and warrants close attention
2. ~$190M/year inter-company satellite-lease payments to EchoStar — a separate creditor group has already retained Glenn Agre Bergman & Fuentes to scrutinize that stream for fraudulent transfer exposure
3. Potential 363 sale of the consumer broadband book (~641,000 residential subscribers, down 21.7% YoY)
4. UCC formation — expected within 21 days of petition date under § 1102

Also on the calendar: Harvest Sherwood Food Distributors confirmation hearing, Bankr. N.D. Tex. (Judge Jernigan), Wednesday August 5 — the U.S. Trustee's opt-out release objection puts post-*Purdue* plan-release standards directly at issue.

Practical takeaway: If you hold Hughes bonds or have an unsecured claim, monitor the first-day hearing for DIP terms and any disclosure on the inter-company lease — those two items will shape recoveries before a plan even takes shape.
