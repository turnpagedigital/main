⚖️ July 1 at 4:00 p.m. ET is the hard stop for BlockFills creditors.

That's the voting deadline and confirmation objection deadline in *In re Reliz Technology Group Holdings Inc., et al.* (dba BlockFills), Bankr. D. Del., Case No. 26-10371, before Judge Horan. Confirmation hearing follows on July 13.

The central unresolved question: are customer deposits property of the estate or carved-out customer property? Debtors' counsel confirmed in open court that all funds were commingled with company funds. No court ruling on that characterization has issued. If the Debtors' position holds at confirmation, unsecured creditors recover against a pool that includes customer deposits. If a challenger prevails, those assets may be carved out.

The economic picture is stark. Keyrock SA is the $3.25M Successful Bidder for substantially all assets. Petition-date general unsecured obligations run approximately $145M. The gap between those two numbers is the recovery problem — unless the liquidating trust generates material returns from avoidance actions or insider litigation. The Special Committee formed March 12, 2026 holds that authority. Whether the plan releases insider claims or reserves them for trust prosecution is the long-tail variable.

Eleven new docket filings hit the record in the 72 hours ending June 26 (Docket Entries 455–467). Objections may be pending. The record is still being built.

Practical takeaway: If you hold a claim and haven't submitted your ballot or evaluated objection rights — particularly on the customer-property characterization and any insider releases — you have four days. The window closes Tuesday.
