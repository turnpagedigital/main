33 days. That's Sleep Number's full runway from Chapter 11 filing to auction — the fastest-moving major retail bankruptcy of 2026.

Sleep Number filed June 12 in S.D.N.Y. (Case No. 26-11399 (KYP)) with $672.5M in defaulted, accelerated debt against roughly $642M in assets. The stalking horse bid: $415M cash from SNBR Inc., a Sleep Country Canada affiliate, under an APA signed the same day as the petition. Bid deadline is July 8. Auction is July 13.

The UCC formation meeting is June 22 — this Sunday — at 12:00 p.m. ET. That committee will have exactly 16 days after it seats to evaluate the stalking horse, challenge bidding procedures, or negotiate any sale-order carve-outs before the bid deadline closes. The debt-to-asset gap means unsecured recoveries hinge entirely on which liabilities the buyer assumes and what administrative claims consume first.

The company ran a 14-week prepetition marketing process — 53 parties contacted, 19 NDAs, five preliminary proposals, one non-actionable recapitalization response. The stalking horse is the market.

Any unsecured creditor with a material claim should contact the U.S. Trustee Region 2 or debtors' counsel Brian Resnick at Davis Polk (212-510-0500) before June 22 to seek Committee appointment. Waiting until after the auction is too late to matter.
