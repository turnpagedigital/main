$529.5M in cash is clearing the Sleep Number estate — and secured lenders holding $672.5M have first claim on every dollar.

The § 363 sale to Sleep Country Canada was approved July 20 (Case No. 26-11399, Bankr. S.D.N.Y., Hon. Kyu Y. Paek). Closing is targeted July 31. The auction moved the price from a $415M stalking horse to ~$701M — a meaningful competitive result. But the gap between the cash component and the secured stack leaves unsecured creditors — trade vendors, warranty counterparties, employees — waiting on math that hasn't been done publicly yet.

What's unresolved as of today:
• No wind-down budget filed
• No claims bar date set
• No liquidating trust framework on the docket
• DIP payoff and administrative expense claims will be deducted before any unsecured distribution

The § 341 meeting ran July 24 by Zoom. Davis Polk (Brian Resnick) is debtor's counsel. Claims are filed through Kroll at restructuring.ra.kroll.com/SleepNumber.

Meanwhile, BlockFills (Case No. 26-10371, Bankr. D. Del.) passed its July 13 confirmation hearing and is generating active post-confirmation docket entries as of July 23. The unresolved question there — whether customer digital assets are estate property or segregated — will drive recovery for ~$145M in unsecured claims.

Practical takeaway: If you hold an unsecured Sleep Number claim, file or confirm with Kroll now. No bar date has been set, but one will follow closing fast. Don't wait for the notice.
