Monday, July 13 at 10:00 a.m. ET: two courtrooms, two creditor disasters on simultaneous timelines.

In *In re Sleep Number Corporation* (No. 26-11399, Bankr. S.D.N.Y.), the § 363 auction opens before Judge Paek at 450 Lexington Avenue. The stalking horse is $415M cash from SNBR Inc. The funded debt is ~$672.5M. The gap is roughly $257.5M — absorbed entirely by secured lenders before unsecured creditors see a dollar. A competing bid must clear $441.45M just to displace the stalking horse. The DIP roll-up — $195M of prepetition debt converted at 3:1 into DIP priority — made that gap structural, not accidental. Post-auction objection deadline: July 14, 4:00 p.m. ET. Sale hearing: July 15, 10:00 a.m. ET.

Same morning, same hour: *In re Reliz Technology Group Holdings Inc.* (No. 26-10371, Bankr. D. Del.) confirmation hearing before Judge Horan. Keyrock SA paid $3.25M for substantially all BlockFills assets. General unsecured claims: ~$145M. Sub-3-cent recovery before administrative costs. The only meaningful upside for unsecured creditors is D&O causes of action preserved in the liquidating trust — whether those claims were actually preserved will be central to any objection Monday. General claims bar date: September 11, 2026, 4:00 p.m. ET.

Practical takeaway: if you hold a lease rejection claim, trade claim, or warranty claim in Sleep Number, counsel needs to be monitoring the auction Monday and positioned to file by the July 14 post-auction deadline — the July 15 sale order will bind non-objecting parties on every assumed contract cure amount.
