# Chapter 11 — Social Posts | Tuesday, August 04, 2026

## LinkedIn

$1.5 billion in maturing notes came due August 3. Hughes Satellite Systems Corporation had $61.2 million in cash.

Hughes and 11 affiliated entities filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions in Bankr. S.D. Tex. (Case No. 26-90739, Judge Perez) on the maturity date itself — a free-fall case with no pre-negotiated plan. White & Case is debtor counsel; FTI's Robert Del Genio is CRO.

The subscriber picture frames the valuation fight ahead:
• Broadband base: 1.56M at end of 2020 → 641,000 by petition date
• Revenue mix shifting toward enterprise, defense, and in-flight — but the consumer collapse is already baked in

Eleven days before the petition, an ad hoc group holding ~80% of the senior bonds by face value (Jones Day counsel) served a formal fraudulent transfer notice. That notice survives the filing and will complicate any inter-debtor claims against EchoStar — which closed a $23 billion spectrum sale to AT&T on July 28, just days before Hughes hit the wall.

This is the second EchoStar subsidiary in Chapter 11 in roughly five weeks. Dish Wireless had a prepack and a signed RSA. Hughes has neither.

For creditors: the fraudulent transfer notice is the most consequential pre-petition document in this case. Track whether the ad hoc group moves quickly to formalize an official committee seat or pursues a parallel adversary track — that choice will shape the valuation litigation timeline.
