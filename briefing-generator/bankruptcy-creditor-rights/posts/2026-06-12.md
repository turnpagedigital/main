# Bankruptcy Creditor Rights — Social Posts | June 12, 2026

## LinkedIn

Two creditor-rights deadlines worth watching this week, both about who actually gets paid.

June 15 — the Cook en banc petition deadline at the Fourth Circuit, in the fight over whether a Chapter 13 standing trustee keeps the percentage fees collected on cases dismissed or converted before confirmation. It sounds technical; it isn't. In the highest-volume corner of the bankruptcy system, that fee question directly shrinks or grows what flows to creditors.

Pending — the ConvergeOne § 1123(a)(4) appeal at the Fifth Circuit, still the most consequential live issue in restructuring. Judge Hanen reversed a prepackaged plan because exclusive backstop and equity-subscription rights handed the majority lenders ~30% higher recoveries than minority lenders in the same class. Unlike Serta, this one is grounded in the Code itself — you can't draft around it. The compliance rule it implies is binary: offer the backstop to everyone in the class, or run a real competitive marketing process before the RSA is signed.

If you're structuring an RSA-based prepack right now, that's the line to stay on the right side of.
