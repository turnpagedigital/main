July 8 is doing a lot of work in the Sleep Number case.

*In re Sleep Number Corporation, et al.*, No. 26-11399 (KYP), Bankr. S.D.N.Y. — the qualified-bid deadline and the § 341 meeting of creditors land on the same day. That collision is not accidental scheduling; it is a structural compression that limits what unsecured creditors can learn before the July 13 auction and July 15 sale-approval hearing close the window.

Key facts on the record:
• $672.5M in prepetition debt, accelerated and in default
• $415M stalking-horse bid from SNBR, Inc. (Sleep Country Canada affiliate), signed June 12
• $260M DIP facility — $65M new money, $195M rollup of prepetition secured debt (3-to-1 ratio)
• 572-store U.S. retail footprint; landlords of rejected stores face § 502(b)(6) cap on rejection damages
• Creditor matrix lists only 50–99 creditors against a $672M liability stack — watch for amendments adding warranty and gift card holders

The lien-investigation budget is capped at $100,000. The challenge deadline runs 60 days from committee formation. If the committee was seated in the days after petition, that clock likely expires on or before the July 15 sale hearing. A § 506(c) surcharge waiver in the interim DIP order would compound the problem.

**Practical takeaway:** UCC members should confirm today whether committee counsel has a plan to preserve lien-challenge and § 506(c) rights before the sale hearing forecloses them. The July 8 § 341 examination is the last realistic opportunity to surface collateral-valuation facts before the auction.
