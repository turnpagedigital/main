July 13 is the fulcrum date for two estates this week.

At BlockFills (Bankr. D. Del. 26-10371), the confirmation hearing is on at 10:00 a.m. ET before Judge Horan. Voting closed July 1. Objection deadline closed July 1. The window is shut for any party that did not file timely — whatever Judge Horan confirms will bind them. The live question at that hearing is whether commingled customer digital assets are estate property under § 541 or customer property subject to tracing. Debtors' position: everything is estate property. Customer claimants who believe otherwise needed an adversary proceeding under FRBP 7001(b). If one wasn't filed, that argument is procedurally foreclosed. Against a $145M general unsecured pool and a $3.25M Keyrock sale price, plan recovery math is not favorable without a successful property dispute.

At Sleep Number (Bankr. S.D.N.Y. 26-11399), the sale objection deadline is July 10 — three business days away. The stalking horse is $415M (SNBR Inc., a Sleep Country Canada subsidiary). Prepetition funded debt is $672.5M. That's a $257.5M gap. No structural path to unsecured recovery unless auction bids clear the full secured stack. Pre-marketing ran 14 weeks, 53 parties, five preliminary proposals. Competing bids are possible, not probable.

Practical note: landlords with 572 store locations across the U.S. should file sale objections or open lease rejection damages negotiations before July 10 — waiting for the sale order is too late.
