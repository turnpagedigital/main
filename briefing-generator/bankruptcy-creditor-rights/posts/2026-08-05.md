# Chapter 11 — Social Posts | Wednesday, August 05, 2026

## LinkedIn

$1.5 billion in funded debt, no RSA, and a creditor count between 10,001 and 25,000. That is the opening position in Hughes Satellite Systems Corporation's free-fall Chapter 11, filed August 2 in the Southern District of Texas (No. 26-90739, Judge Perez).

The structure: two equal tranches — $750M of 5.250% senior secured notes and $750M of 6.625% senior unsecured notes, both indentures dated July 27, 2016, both matured August 1, 2026. The company filed August 2, one day before the indenture's holiday-adjusted payment deadline. No restructuring support agreement. No DIP confirmed at filing. White & Case is debtor counsel; FTI is financial advisor.

The operational story is blunt. Subscribers fell from 1.56 million at year-end 2020 to roughly 641,000 by filing date — Starlink competition is the stated cause. Cash on hand: $102 million against that debt wall. The reorganization thesis is a pivot to enterprise, defense, and in-flight contracts. Whether that contract book supports the secured stack is the contested valuation question.

This follows EchoStar subsidiary Dish Wireless's prepackaged filing on June 30, 2026. Hughes was deliberately kept outside that process. The sequencing protected the AT&T spectrum transaction first; now the satellite broadband unit faces its own contested restructuring.

The $750M/$750M face-amount parity between secured and unsecured sets up a classic inter-creditor fight. Watch creditors' committee formation, any DIP motion, and early scheduling from Judge Perez.

Practical note for claimants: with no RSA and no consensual roadmap, plan confirmation is months away at minimum. Document your claims carefully and monitor the committee appointment — that is your primary advocacy channel in the near term.
