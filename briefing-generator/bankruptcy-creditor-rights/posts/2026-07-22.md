Judge Paek entered the Sleep Number sale order July 20. The clock to July 31 close is now running.

The arithmetic for unsecured creditors hasn't improved. SNBR Inc. — Sleep Country Canada's subsidiary — remains the buyer at roughly $415 million, no competing bid surfaced. Set that against $672.5 million in secured debt and the gap before the waterfall reaches unsecured claims is $257.5 million. The DIP structure makes it worse: $195 million of the $260 million facility is a roll-up, converting prepetition debt to superpriority administrative status. That sits ahead of every unsecured claim.

Key dates still live on this docket:
• § 341 creditors' meeting — July 24, 2026, 1:00 p.m. ET, by Zoom
• Sale close target — July 31, 2026
• Automatic stay relief hearing — August 20, 2026, 10:00 a.m. ET

Once the sale closes, the residual estate moves to wind-down fast. Administrative expense claimants — post-petition vendors, suppliers — get priority in that queue. Prepetition unsecured creditors are behind them.

Practical takeaway: If you supplied goods or services post-petition, confirm administrative expense treatment now, before an administrative bar date enters. If your prepetition claim isn't scheduled, file a proof of claim before any general bar date is set. Check restructuring.ra.kroll.com/SleepNumber for filings. Case: *In re Sleep Number Corp.*, No. 26-11399, Bankr. S.D.N.Y.
