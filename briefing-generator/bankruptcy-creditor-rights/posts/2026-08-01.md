$400M+ in general unsecured claims. 100,000+ creditors. A liquidating posture. That's the RNDC estate as it sits today.

Republic National Distributing Company filed July 26 in S.D. Tex. (Judge Lopez) alongside 17 affiliates. No new docket activity overnight — the case is still in its opening phase. The next substantive checkpoint is the second-day hearing: **August 24, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. CT**.

A few numbers worth keeping in view:

• Liabilities: $1B–$10B against $500M–$1B in assets
• Unsecured pool: $400M+ estimated GUCs
• Creditor count: 100,000+
• Largest single unsecured claim: Proximo Spirits at $93.9M
• Supplier defections from 2022–2025 erased more than $3B in annual revenue

This is not a reorganization. Kirkland & Ellis, Porter Hedges, AlixPartners (CRO: John Castellano), and Lazard (Christian Tempke) are running a supervised wind-down with market sales where viable. Roughly 700 suppliers had already transitioned to new distributors before the petition date.

For distressed buyers: with claims this dispersed, aggregation and secondary-market positioning ahead of any 363 sale proceeds distribution are the primary recovery levers. August 24 is where the practical timeline starts to take shape.
