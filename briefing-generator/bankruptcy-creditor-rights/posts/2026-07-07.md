## LinkedIn

4:00 p.m. ET today is the bid deadline in *In re Sleep Number Corporation* (No. 26-11399, Bankr. S.D.N.Y., Judge Paek).

The arithmetic is stark. The stalking horse — SNBR Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Sleep Country Canada — bid $415 million in cash under an APA dated June 12. Funded debt at the petition date: approximately $672.5 million in aggregate principal, all in default and accelerated. That's a ~62-cent recovery against secured claims before a dollar reaches unsecured creditors.

The debtors have indicated funds will be available for unsecured distribution. How much depends entirely on whether competing bids materialize before today's cutoff and what price clears at the auction Sunday, July 13 (10:00 a.m. ET).

Key dates for anyone in the capital structure:
• Bid deadline — Today, July 7 at 4:00 p.m. ET
• § 341 meeting — Today at 1:00 p.m. ET (Zoom)
• Sale objections due — July 10 at 4:00 p.m. ET
• Auction (if required) — July 13 at 10:00 a.m. ET
• Sale hearing before Judge Paek — July 15 at 10:00 a.m. ET
• Second-day hearing — July 8 at 10:00 a.m. ET

Davis Polk (Brian M. Resnick) is lead debtors' counsel. The bidding procedures order entered July 2, compressing post-petition diligence to under two weeks.

Practical takeaway: Unsecured creditors should watch the July 10 objection deadline for committee positioning on distribution mechanics — that filing will be the first public signal of where the committee thinks the waterfall lands.
