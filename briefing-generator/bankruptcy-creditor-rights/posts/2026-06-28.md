July 2 is the gate. Sleep Number's bidding-procedures hearing lands Thursday before Judge Paek (S.D.N.Y. 26-11399), and if those procedures are approved as filed, the July 8 bid deadline becomes binding — leaving roughly six days for any competing buyer to surface on a 572-store retail footprint.

The structure in brief:
• $415M stalking horse from SNBR Inc. (Sleep Country Canada affiliate), signed June 12
• $260M DIP — $65M new money, $195M roll-up, priced SOFR +8%
• 26-day postpetition diligence window; auction July 13, sale hearing July 15, close July 31
• No creditors' committee publicly announced as of this writing
• DIP milestones run concurrently with any committee challenge deadline, compressing the investigation window

The roll-up is the piece worth watching. $195M of prepetition debt gets elevated to DIP priority before a committee even forms. In recent 363s — Enviva, Red Lobster — committees challenged this structure. Without a committee here, that lever may not exist.

HSR clearance is an explicit closing condition per the SEC 8-K, adding regulatory friction to the July 31 hard close.

Break-up fee is 3% plus $4M expense reimbursement — low-end for a deal this size, which modestly improves competing-bidder economics.

Practical takeaway: Unsecured creditors and landlords with rejection exposure on the 44 pending lease rejections should evaluate a bid-procedures objection before July 1. The window to shape this process closes at the Thursday hearing.
