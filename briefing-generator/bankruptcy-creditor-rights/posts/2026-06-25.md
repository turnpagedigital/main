The roll-up is the pressure point. Prepetition obligations convert into superpriority DIP claims ahead of every unsecured creditor in the waterfall. Practical takeaway: If you hold a lease, supply, warranty, or IP claim against Sleep Number, July 2 is your first real window. Counsel should be engaged and prepared to appear before that hearing — the compressed timeline does not pause for late entrants.
