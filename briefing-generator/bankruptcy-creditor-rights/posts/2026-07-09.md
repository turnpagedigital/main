The commingling disclosure is the number that matters most for GUC recoveries. Debtors' counsel confirmed at the first-day hearing that customer funds were commingled with company and other customer funds, and the debtors' position is that all such assets are estate property — not customer property. On January 16, 2026, 40.001 BTC and 650,050 USDC transferred by Alberta were swept from the deposit wallet within minutes. If the court adopts the estate-property characterization, tracing arguments fail and the full customer shortfall folds into the pro rata GUC pool.

Judge Horan did enforce recoupment rights in May (Dkt. 357, Ward Trust — $2.8M GUC claim allowed after netting a $7.224M loan against BTC collateral). That ruling is fact-specific and does not disturb the broader commingling question.

Practical takeaway: If you hold a BlockFills claim, confirm today that your proof of claim appears on the voting register as properly scheduled or temporarily allowed under the May 28 solicitation order (Dkt. 388). With four days to confirmation, there is no curative runway left.
