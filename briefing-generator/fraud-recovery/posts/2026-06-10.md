## LinkedIn

$3.59 million on hand. $140 million in claims.

That's the math the First Liberty Building & Loan receiver laid out to defrauded investors — and it's the single best argument for why recovery in a Ponzi case is never about the cash in the estate's bank account.

The real recovery comes from three places:
1. Clawbacks against net winners — the investors who pulled out more than they put in. Those "fictitious profits" are stolen principal, and receivers can recover them for the common pool.
2. Enabler liability — Bankers Life already agreed to repay ~$7M to First Liberty investors.
3. Criminal restitution — prosecutors charged founder Edwin Brant Frost IV in April, opening a second recovery track.

Meanwhile the pipeline of new receiverships keeps filling: the $328M Goliath Ventures crypto fraud (receiver appointed March 2026), the $400M Paramount/Heller scheme (~2,700 investors), and the $191M Agridime cattle-contract case.

If you're a defrauded investor, the move is the same in every one: document your net investment and file your claim early. Pro-rata distributions are built on the claim you perfect now.
