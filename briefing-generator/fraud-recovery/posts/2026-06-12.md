## LinkedIn

A Chicago Ponzi case hits its first courtroom test this morning, and it's a clinic in parallel-track enforcement.

John Sterling Myers is arraigned at 10:00 a.m. today before Judge Edmond Chang (N.D. Ill.) on four counts of wire fraud — one week after the SEC filed its civil complaint. The scheme: ~$4M raised from at least 28 investors across five states, fabricated quarterly statements showing 16%–54% returns, and, when redemptions came due in spring 2025, a false claim that the SEC had "frozen" the fund.

The recovery problem is structural. None of the three Sterling entities was ever registered as an investment adviser. That means no SIPC protection and no FINRA arbitration path. Victim recovery depends entirely on the SEC disgorgement pool plus criminal restitution — and only ~$398K has been repaid against $4M raised.

Practice note: the arraignment is the moment an asset-freeze or receiver motion is most likely to land. If you represent victims, watch the docket today and be ready to file protectively before the disgorgement pool is fixed.

Sentencing in the separate $160M A.G. Morgan / Camarda fraud is set for August before Judge Choudhury (E.D.N.Y.).
