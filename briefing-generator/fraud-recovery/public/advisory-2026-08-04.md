**Next scheduled milestone to watch:**

 Those deadlines are the next hard events with pricing relevance for claim buyers. At current asset disclosure —

In *Power Block Coin*, 
the Official Committee of Unsecured Creditors, represented by Greenberg Traurig, has filed a liquidation plan addressing over $192 million in claims, proposing to wind down the debtor under Chapter 11 and establish an independent Liquidating Trust.
 
A $17.4 million loan to Solara Communities LLC — an affiliate developing real property in Toquerville, Utah — remains under scrutiny given its unsecured status and recent encumbrances on the underlying property.
 Confirmation-track progress and resolution of the Solara loan are the next substantive triggers in that case.
