# 🕵️ Ponzi / Fraud Recovery | Monday, June 08, 2026

## Analysis & Developments

**Today's deltas:** The Southern District of Florida's FTX-related third-party fraudulent-transfer litigation produced a significant scheduling order late Friday; the SEC's clawback action against a multi-state Ponzi operator yielded a stipulated preliminary injunction preserving an estimated $47 million in frozen assets; and FINRA's arbitration panel in New York entered a default award against a defunct GPB Capital affiliate. Carry-forward matters include the Madoff BLMIS estate's twenty-fourth interim distribution, the OneCoin / Ruja Ignatova forfeiture proceedings, ongoing CFTC enforcement against Digitex Futures, and the transnational ZeekRewards estate wind-down. Each is addressed in turn.

### I. FTX Estate — Fraudulent-Transfer Litigation & Third-Party Recoveries

The FTX Debtors' Third-Party Fraudulent-Transfer Adversary Proceedings, pending before Judge John T. Dorsey, U.S. Bankruptcy Court, District of Delaware (Lead Case No. 22-11068-JTD), continued to generate calendar activity heading into the week. On Friday, June 6, 2026, Judge Dorsey entered a scheduling order in *FTX Trading Ltd. v. Sequoia Capital Operations LLC et al.* (Adv. No. 23-50697-JTD) setting a fact-discovery cutoff of September 12, 2026 and an expert-report deadline of November 7, 2026 (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.law360.com/bankruptcy/articles/ftx-fraudulent-transfer-scheduling)__). The Sequoia adversary, which seeks recovery of approximately $213 million in pre-petition transfers characterized as constructively fraudulent under 11 U.S.C. §§ 548 and 550, is one of roughly 40 parallel avoidance actions the Debtors filed against venture-capital and hedge-fund counterparties beginning in late 2023 (__[Source — needs verification](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/ftx-debtors-adversary-proceedings-tracker)__).

Separately, the estate's litigation trust — established under the confirmed Third Amended Joint Plan of Reorganization (Docket No. 26404, confirmed October 7, 2025) — reported an aggregate $9.4 billion recovered-or-committed figure as of the most recent monthly operating report filed May 30, 2026 (Docket No. 31882). Plan Administrators John J. Ray III and the Sullivan & Cromwell team have represented that a fifth interim distribution of between $0.80 and $1.10 per dollar of allowed claims in Class 5A (non-governmental unsecured) is projected for Q4 2026, contingent on the resolution of Bahamas liquidator competing-claim issues and at least partial settlement of the Sequoia and Paradigm adversaries (__[Source — needs verification](https://restructuring.ra.kroll.com/FTX)__). Creditor counsel tracking the Bahamas-concurrent-proceeding dispute should monitor Joint Provisional Liquidators' application pending before the Supreme Court of the Bahamas (In the Matter of FTX Digital Markets Ltd., Cause No. 2022/COM/00001) — a scheduling conference is set for June 18, 2026, and the comity question of whether U.S. distributions can proceed absent a parallel recognition protocol under UNCITRAL Model Law Chapter XV remains live (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.bahamaslawreports.com/ftx-jpl-2026)__).

### II. SEC Clawback Action — Multistate Ponzi Operator (Operation Bright Margin)

On June 5, 2026, the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission filed *SEC v. Castellano et al.*, Case No. 1:26-cv-04817 (S.D.N.Y., Judge Analisa Torres), alleging that defendants Anthony Castellano and Meridian Yield Partners LLC operated a $112 million Ponzi scheme targeting retail investors in at least nine states, predominantly through investment contracts in a purported "AI-driven arbitrage fund" — instruments deemed securities under *SEC v. Howey Co.*, 328 U.S. 293 (1946) (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.sec.gov/litigation/litreleases/2026/lr26400.htm)__). The Complaint alleges violations of Securities Act §§ 5, 17(a); Exchange Act § 10(b) and Rule 10b-5 thereunder; and Investment Advisers Act §§ 206(1) and (2).

On the same date, Judge Torres signed a Temporary Restraining Order freezing approximately $47 million held across six domestic and two offshore accounts at Signature Bank successors and a Cayman-domiciled custodian. A stipulated preliminary injunction preserving those frozen assets through at least August 29, 2026 was entered June 6, 2026 (Docket No. 14). The SEC has moved to appoint Robb Evans & Associates LLC as Receiver — a motion scheduled for hearing on June 16, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. in Courtroom 15C, 500 Pearl Street, New York (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.sec.gov/litigation/litreleases/2026/lr26401.htm)__). If appointed, the Receiver will be empowered under the TRO framework to issue Section 21(e) subpoenas, conduct 2004-equivalent examinations, and pursue avoidance claims against net-winner investors who received distributions in excess of principal — a statutory mechanism confirmed in *S.E.C. v. Byers*, 609 F.3d 87 (2d Cir. 2010) (__[Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/securities-law/sec-ponzi-receiver-authority)__).

Investors who believe they are victims of the Castellano scheme should file a proof of claim with the prospective receivership within 60 days of the Receiver's formal appointment. Net-winner clawback demand letters — a standard Receiver tool under *Cunningham v. Brown*, 265 U.S. 1 (1924) — are expected to go out within 90 days of appointment. Any net-winner recipient who reinvested principal in later rounds may argue for a *good-faith transferee* affirmative defense under 11 U.S.C. § 548(c) by analogy; circuit-level precedent in the Second Circuit (*In re Bayou Group*, 439 B.R. 284 (Bankr. S.D.N.Y. 2010)) requires the recipient to demonstrate objective commercially reasonable reliance (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/bayou-group-439-br-284)__).

### III. GPB Capital — FINRA Default Award & Ongoing Broker-Dealer Liability

On June 4, 2026, a FINRA Dispute Resolution arbitration panel in New York (FINRA Case No. 23-01884) entered a default award of $2.2 million against GPB Capital Holdings LLC affiliate Ascendant Capital LLC and former registered representative Derek Galanis in favor of four claimants who invested an aggregate $1.85 million in GPB Holdings II LP and GPB Automotive Portfolio LP — both now in Chapter 11 before Judge Harlin D. Hale, U.S. Bankruptcy Court, Northern District of Texas (Case Nos. 21-30071 and 21-30072) (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.finra.org/arbitration-mediation/arbitration-awards/2026/case-23-01884)__). The award — which includes $350,000 in compensatory damages above invested principal, plus $124,000 in attorney's fees — was premised on findings of suitability violations under FINRA Rules 2111 and 2010, and negligent misrepresentation under New York common law.

For claimants who invested through broker-dealers other than Ascendant, arbitration windows remain open against at least fifteen additional registered representatives identified in the SEC's parallel enforcement action, *SEC v. GPB Capital Holdings*, No. 1:21-cv-00583 (E.D.N.Y., Judge Ann Donnelly) (__[Source — needs verification](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/securities-law/gpb-capital-sec-action-update-2026)__). The six-year FINRA eligibility clock under Rule 12206 continues to run from the date of each claimant's final investment, meaning investors whose last contribution predates June 2020 may already be time-barred absent a tolling argument. Recovery counsel should assess whether the SEC's January 2021 complaint filing triggers an equitable-tolling ground under *American Pipe & Construction Co. v. Utah*, 414 U.S. 538 (1974) — a question not yet squarely decided in the FINRA context (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.gtlaw.com/insights/gpb-capital-finra-tolling-analysis)__).

### IV. BLMIS / Madoff Estate — Twenty-Fourth Interim Distribution & Irving Picard Recovery Pace

Irving H. Picard, Trustee for the liquidation of Bernard L. Madoff Investment Securities LLC under the Securities Investor Protection Act (SIPA), Case No. 08-01789 (Bankr. S.D.N.Y., Judge Cecelia G. Morris, with references on specific adversaries to Judge Michael E. Wiles), has recovered approximately **$14.77 billion** through the close of May 2026 — representing roughly **88.4%** of the $16.71 billion in allowed net equity claims by direct account holders (__[SIPC — Madoff Recovery Initiative](https://www.madofftrustee.com/distribution.html)__). The twenty-fourth interim distribution, announced May 29, 2026, will deliver a pro-rata payout of approximately 2.3% of each claimant's allowed net equity claim, with an aggregate distribution amount of approximately $385 million. Checks and wire transfers are expected to issue by July 11, 2026.

The Trustee's pending litigation pipeline includes 23 active adversary proceedings seeking approximately $1.1 billion in aggregate, predominantly against net-winner feeder-fund entities domiciled in the British Virgin Islands and Luxembourg. Notably, *Picard v. Fairfield Sentry Ltd. (In Liquidation)* (Adv. No. 09-01239) remains in post-settlement compliance monitoring; the $235 million Fairfield settlement approved in 2021 is being funded in installments through 2026 (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.madofftrustee.com/fairfield-sentry.html)__). Counsel representing indirect Madoff investors who received payments through feeder funds should advise their clients that SIPA net-equity calculations do not recognize fictional paper profits — a rule reaffirmed by the Second Circuit in *In re Bernard L. Madoff Inv. Sec. LLC*, 654 F.3d 229 (2d Cir. 2011), cert. denied — and that any residual direct claims for the fictional-profits differential remain permanently disallowed (__[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/madoff-trustee-distribution-2026)__).

### V. OneCoin / Ruja Ignatova — Forfeiture & Extraterritorial Recovery

The forfeiture proceeding ancillary to *United States v. Ignatova*, No. 1:17-cr-00630-MKB (E.D.N.Y., Judge Margo K. Brodie), continues without the defendant, who remains a fugitive on Interpol Red Notice and was added to the FBI's Ten Most Wanted list in June 2022. As of June 2026, the DOJ's Money Laundering and Asset Recovery Section (MLARS) has seized or restrained approximately $\mathbf{2.1 billion}$ in OneCoin-linked assets globally — including cryptocurrency wallets, luxury real estate in Dubai, and brokerage accounts in Frankfurt — through Letters Rogatory requests to Germany, the UAE, and Cayman Islands authorities (__[DOJ — Asset Forfeiture](https://www.justice.gov/criminal-mlars/case-updates/onecoin)__). Co-conspirator Sebastian Greenwood (sentenced to 20 years in September 2023) and Karl Sebastian Greenwood's forfeiture judgment of $300 million remains substantially uncollected, with MLARS counsel indicating that UAE asset-tracing efforts are ongoing under the 2024 U.S.-UAE mutual legal assistance treaty addendum (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.justice.gov/criminal-mlars/pr/2024-uae-mlat-addendum)__).

Potential victim recovery through the federal Crime Victims' Rights Act (18 U.S.C. § 3771) and the Mandatory Victims Restitution Act (18 U.S.C. § 3663A) depends on Ignatova's eventual apprehension and conviction. In the interim, victims who purchased OneCoins through EU-based distributors should monitor the Bulgarian National Investigation Service's parallel asset-freeze orders — approximately €312 million in Bulgarian-domiciled assets remain frozen under Bulgarian Criminal Procedure Code Article 72 — and file timely claims with the Bulgarian State Agency for National Security (DANS) victim registry, whose next submission window closes September 30, 2026 (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.dans.bg/en/onecoin-victim-registry)__).

### VI. ZeekRewards — Transnational Estate Wind-Down

The ZeekRewards receivership, *Bell v. Disner et al.*, No. 3:12-cv-00519-GCM (W.D.N.C., Judge Graham C. Mullen), administered by Receiver Kenneth D. Bell, is in final wind-down. The Receiver's May 2026 quarterly report reflects total recoveries of approximately $285 million against a claimant pool of roughly 860,000 net-loser investors worldwide. The final distribution round — expected to be the eighth and last — is projected to yield between $0.07 and $0.09 on the dollar for allowed claims, with a distribution date estimated for late Q3 2026 (__[Source — needs verification](https://www.zeekrewardsreceivership.com/quarterly-reports/q1-2026)__). Claimants who have not yet updated their payment information with the Claims Administrator (Gilardi & Co. LLC) are urged to do so before July 15, 2026, to avoid diversion of their distribution to a residual pool (__[ZeekRewards Receivership — Claims Portal](https://www.zeekrewardsreceivership.com)__).
