### *In re Goliath Ventures, Inc.* — No. 26-13174-RAM (Bankr. S.D. Fla.) · Judge Robert A. Mark


Goliath Ventures, Inc. — formerly doing business as Gen-Z Venture Firm Inc. — was placed into receivership under Michael S. Budwick before its Chapter 11 petition was filed on March 16, 2026, in the Southern District of Florida.
 
The DOJ arrested founder Christopher Alexander Delgado on February 24, 2026 on wire fraud and money laundering charges, alleging a Ponzi scheme that ran from January 2023 through January 2026 and defrauded investors of at least $328 million.


The tracked-case ground truth records the most recent docket activity as Entry 22, filed July 28, 2026 — a Disclosure of Compensation — with zero new entries in the 72-hour window ending August 2. 
Prior to this quiet stretch, the debtor had filed a Motion to Extend Exclusivity Period for Filing a Chapter 11 Plan and Disclosure Statement (Dkt. 187) and a Motion for Authority to Use a Single DIP Operating Account for Both Jointly Administered Estates (Dkt. 188), with hearings on both scheduled for July 9, 2026 by video conference before Judge Mark.
 Whether those motions were granted is not yet confirmed in today's sources; claims investors should treat plan-filing exclusivity as the controlling near-term variable.

On the asset-recovery side, 
Judge Mark entered an order on May 19, 2026 (Dkt. 133) approving a compromise and settlement with the Orlando Economic Partnership, requiring OEP to pay $190,000 to the debtor estate within ten calendar days of the order becoming final and non-appealable.
 That settlement is not the story today — it's background confirming the estate is actively pursuing third-party recoveries. 
Delgado had previously been ordered as a condition of pretrial release to repatriate funds held in the firm's Dubai accounts and to surrender jewelry, vehicles, and other tangible assets to the federal government by the end of April 2026.
 Whether those repatriation obligations have been satisfied — or whether the estate has moved to enforce them — is not confirmed today.


The filing indicates assets of $1 million to $10 million against liabilities of $100 million to $500 million
, a structural deficit that frames clawback exposure under 11 U.S.C. § 548 as the primary recovery lever for net-winner creditors. 
The top 20 unsecured creditors are almost all potential victims of fraud by Christopher A. Delgado, with Gregory Wilson listed at $8.7 million and other creditors in the hundreds of thousands.
 The Ponzi-presumption doctrine — which treats any transfer from a Ponzi operator as made with actual fraudulent intent, satisfying § 548(a)(1)(A) without a subjective intent showing — applies straightforwardly on these facts; net winners above their principal investment face real clawback exposure at whatever recovery ceiling the estate can reach.

**Next milestone to watch:** An exclusivity ruling from Judge Mark (outcome of the July 9 hearing not yet confirmed) and any disclosure statement filing deadline set thereafter. Claim trading in this case is pricing both the Dubai repatriation upside and the third-party bank and exchange litigation that remains active.

### *In re Power Block Coin, LLC* — No. 24-23041 (Bankr. D. Utah) · Judge Cathleen D. Parker

The tracked-case ground truth records the most recent filing as July 23, 2026, with zero new entries in the 72-hour window. No docket movement today.


The Official Committee of Unsecured Creditors filed a liquidation plan in the Bankruptcy Court for the District of Utah (Case No. 24-bk-23041-JTM), prepared by Greenberg Traurig, LLP on January 30, 2025, establishing a Liquidating Trust overseen by an independent trustee.
 
The plan addresses claims totaling over $192 million, including claims from Celsius Network LLC exceeding $133 million.
 The July 23 filing (Dkt. 532 — Complaint) is the most recent substantive entry per the ground truth; the nature and defendants of that adversary complaint are not confirmed in today's sources and will require direct docket verification.

A note on the judge assignment: the tracked-case ground truth lists Judge Cathleen D. Parker; earlier case filings and court records reflect Judge Joel T. Marker as the original assigned judge. 
Power Block Coin filed its voluntary Chapter 11 petition on June 20, 2024, with the case initially assigned as No. 24-23041 before Judge Joel T. Marker in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court, District of Utah, Salt Lake City division.
 The reassignment to Judge Parker reflected in the tracked-case ground truth is authoritative; no further verification of the reassignment date is available today.

**Next milestone to watch:** Resolution of the liquidation plan — confirmation hearing date not yet confirmed in today's sources — and any rulings on adversary proceedings flowing from the July 23 complaint (Dkt. 532). The Celsius preference exposure ($133M+) remains the single largest variable in distribution modeling for non-Celsius creditors.
