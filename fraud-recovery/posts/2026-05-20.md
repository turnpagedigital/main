**Single theme:** Net-winner clawback defense — the inquiry-notice good-faith standard, why it must be built before the demand arrives, and why *Goliath Ventures* is two months into the window.

## LinkedIn

The most consequential decision a net-winner investor will make in a Ponzi recovery is the one made before any clawback demand arrives. The window to make it well is the 12 to 18 months between the receiver's appointment and the receiver's first clawback motion. *Goliath Ventures*, in receivership in Broward County (Florida) Circuit Court since March 3, 2026 against a $328 million alleged crypto-investment fraud, is two months into that window.

The doctrinal frame is the inquiry-notice standard for the good-faith defense under fraudulent-transfer law. A net winner who took out more than they put in must, to defend a clawback, demonstrate both subjective good faith and the absence of facts that would have prompted a reasonable investor to inquire further. The bar has risen meaningfully in the last decade. The Second Circuit's *In re Bernard L. Madoff Investment Securities LLC* line has narrowed the good-faith defense, made the Ponzi-scheme presumption available even where Section 546(e) blocks constructive-fraud theories, and rejected the "futility exception" — the argument that an inquiry would not have surfaced the fraud anyway.

The practical translation: documentation built before a demand arrives is the defense. Documentation built after the demand looks defensive on its face and is weighted accordingly. The categories that matter are (1) the contemporaneous record of how the investment opportunity was sourced and what diligence the investor actually performed, (2) the full file of account statements and distribution records, (3) the correspondence trail showing how the investor responded to any red flags — third-party warnings, refused redemptions, unusual return profiles — that the receiver will later argue should have triggered inquiry, and (4) the tax basis records that establish what was contributed against what was withdrawn.

Net-winner investors in Goliath Ventures should be building that file now, while the contemporaneous record is still accessible and memory is still fresh. The receiver's first wave of clawback motions in cases of this scale typically lands 12 to 18 months post-appointment, putting the *Goliath Ventures* clawback window in the March-to-September 2027 range. The documentation built in the next four months is the defense that will be deployed eighteen months from now.
