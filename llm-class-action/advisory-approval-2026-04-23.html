Today's Briefing
Today was the original Bartz v. Anthropic fairness hearing date before Judge Martínez-Olguín rescheduled to May 14. Class counsel cut their fee request by 37.5% — from $300M to $187.5M (12.5% of the fund) — increasing the net amount available for the 440,490 claimed works. Concord Music's First Amended Complaint now targets CEO Dario Amodei and co-founder Benjamin Mann personally for contributory infringement, following the Supreme Court's unanimous Cox Communications reversal. On Capitol Hill, the 25th GRAMMYS on the Hill concludes today championing the TRAIN Act, CLEAR Act, and NO FAKES Act — three bills that would reshape AI companies' disclosure and compliance obligations regardless of how courts resolve pending fair-use motions.
