Book, News, and Journal Publishers Join with Authors in Amicus Brief in Support of Music Publishers in Concord v. Anthropic Legal
AAP  ·  April 2026
AAP, News/Media Alliance, STM, and Authors Guild filed amicus brief supporting music publishers' partial summary judgment motion against Anthropic. This cross-industry coalition — spanning music, books, news, and academic publishing — represents the broadest rights-holder alignment in any AI copyright case and strengthens the market-harm argument under the fourth fair-use factor of 17 U.S.C. § 107.
RIAA, NMPA, and More File Amicus Brief Backing UMG, Concord and ABKCO in Original Anthropic Case Legal
Music Business Worldwide  ·  April 2026
RIAA, NMPA, A2IM, SoundExchange, SONA, and allied organizations filed amicus brief calling Anthropic's unlicensed copying "inexcusable," citing 2026 academic research showing LLMs memorize and can reproduce copyrighted texts — directly challenging AI companies' claims that training data is not retained in models.
Warner Music Group Strikes 'Landmark' Deal with Suno; Settles Copyright Lawsuit Against AI Music Generator Settlement
Music Business Worldwide  ·  November 2025
WMG's settlement with Suno establishes the first comprehensive AI music licensing template: Suno must deprecate current unlicensed models, launch licensed models in 2026, restrict downloads to paid accounts, and give artists full NIL control. Combined with UMG/WMG-Udio settlements, creates a market-rate pricing framework undermining fair-use defenses in pending cases.
Copyright and AI Remain in Focus for 2026 with Getty Appeal Given the Green Light Doctrine
Chambers and Partners  ·  January 2026
Getty Images granted permission to appeal the UK High Court's ruling that AI model weights ≠ "infringing copies" under CDPA § 27. The Court of Appeal proceedings will produce the first UK appellate ruling on AI copyright — with potential divergence from the German GEMA v. OpenAI judgment finding AI memorization constitutes infringement under EU law.
CLEAR Act Would Establish Notice Requirements for Copyrighted Works in AI Training Data Policy
IP Watchdog  ·  February 2026
Detailed analysis of the bipartisan CLEAR Act (S. 3813): mandatory training-data notices to the Copyright Office 30 days before commercial release, $5,000 per-work civil penalties for omissions, and creation of a public database. Retroactive disclosure requirement would force companies to enumerate every copyrighted work in existing datasets, creating unprecedented transparency.
