The financial architecture of the Bartz v. Anthropic settlement has sharpened twenty-two days before the May 14 fairness hearing, with Publishers Weekly reporting a net settlement fund of ~$1.29 billion after ~$208.6 million in costs, yielding an estimated base payout of ~$2,931 per work across 440,490 claimed titles. Anthropic’s second $300 million installment is due April 30. In the Concord v. On the legislative front, Senator Blackburn’s “Trump America AI Act” would legislatively declare AI training is not fair use, clashing with the White House’s court-driven framework and the narrower CLEAR Act’s disclosure-only approach.
