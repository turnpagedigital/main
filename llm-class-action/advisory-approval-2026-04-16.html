Supreme Court Narrows Secondary Copyright Liability in Cox: Implications for AI Companies and Copyright Owners Doctrine
Arnold & Porter  ·  April 2026
The unanimous Cox Communications, Inc. v. Sony Music Entertainment decision (Mar. 25, 2026) reverses the $1B Fourth Circuit verdict and narrows contributory liability to active inducement or services without substantial noninfringing uses. As applied to general-purpose LLMs, the decision functionally closes the "mere knowledge" theory at the entity level while opening an inducement-based route to individual-officer liability.
