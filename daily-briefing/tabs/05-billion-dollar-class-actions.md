# Notes on Tab 06 — $1B+ Class Action Settlements

**Audience:** Plaintiff-side mega-class firms (Robbins Geller, Bernstein Litowitz, Cohen Milstein, Lieff Cabraser, Hagens Berman); defendant-side mega-class defense (Wachtell, S&C, Gibson Dunn, Kirkland); settlement administrators (Epiq, KCC, A.B. Data); litigation funders; corporate GCs at frequent-defendant industries (banks, pharma, big tech, big retail); academic class-action scholars.

**Distinct from adjacent tabs:** Aggregation/curation function more than primary reporting. Many items in this tab will cross-reference primary tabs (e.g., AI copyright settlements cross to Tab 02; antitrust class actions complementing Tab 05 mass arb). The editorial value is the cumulative tracker view across substantive areas.

**Voice:** Litigation-grade with a settlement-economics overlay. Reads like a Cornerstone Research mid-year report or a Stanford Securities Class Action Clearinghouse update. Dollar figures, claims rates, distribution math, fee petitions, fee-shifting orders.

**Recurring sub-themes:**
- BCBS subscriber antitrust at $2.67B (May 2026 payments cycle)
- Bartz v. Anthropic at $1.5B (cross-link Tab 02)
- The annual CFO Dive top-settlements roundup as a calibration benchmark
- Settlement-administrator litigation
- Objector cycles in mega-deals
- Claim rates as a settlement-fairness signal

**Signal test for high urgency:** A new preliminary or final approval of a $1B+ deal, a major objector ruling, a class-certification reversal/affirmance in a putative mega-case, or a fee-petition ruling shifting precedent. Two or more 48h items = "high"; one = "normal"; zero = "quiet."
