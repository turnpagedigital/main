# Notes on Tab 07 — Bankruptcy Creditor Rights

**Audience:** Plan-confirmation litigators, indenture trustees, ad-hoc creditor groups, mass-tort claimants' committees, restructuring partners, DIP lenders, ABL/term-loan defense counsel, Section 363 buyers, restructuring academics.

**Distinct from adjacent tabs:** Episodic appellate-decision-heavy. This tab moves on appellate calendars (Circuit panel rulings, SCOTUS certs) rather than daily docket activity. Editorial cadence will run quieter than Tabs 01-05 but with higher per-item impact.

**Voice:** Restructuring-grade — appellate-court-fluent. Reads like a Jones Day Insights piece, a Weil Briefly Insights note, or an ABI Journal article. Statutory citations and slip-opinion docket numbers naturally integrated. Doctrine taxonomy (cramdown, classification, gerrymandering, releases, mootness, exculpation, Jevic-compliance) used in precise senses.

**Recurring sub-themes:**
- The cross-circuit drift on equitable mootness (4th Cir. narrowing, 5th Cir. bypass, 6th Cir. Ch. 7 carve-out, 3rd Cir. substantial-consummation)
- The post-Purdue third-party release doctrine (which circuits will and won't allow non-consensual non-debtor releases on "exceptional facts")
- The Ninth Circuit broader exculpation track
- The Texas Two-Step / divisional merger ecosystem (3M Aearo, J&J)
- SCOTUS docket watch (cert petitions on plan-confirmation issues)
- Subchapter V threshold changes and case-mix shifts

**Signal test for high urgency:** A published Circuit decision on confirmation, an en banc grant, a SCOTUS cert grant on a Chapter 11 issue, or a Supreme Court argument. One published Circuit decision = "high"; one notable order = "normal"; zero qualifying items = "quiet."
