# Notes on Tab 03 — Crypto Insolvency

**Audience:** Distressed-debt buyers, crypto-creditor committees, restructuring counsel, customer-property litigators, claims-trading firms, in-house counsel at exchanges and lenders.

**Distinct from adjacent tabs:** This tab is restructuring-first. The legal questions are about *how* customer assets are characterized, *who* counts as a creditor, and *what* the plan-confirmation pathway looks like. It is NOT about regulatory enforcement, fraud charges, or recovery against insiders (those go to Tab 04).

**Voice:** Restructuring-grade — reads like a Weil restructuring alert or a Cleary Gottlieb "Global Restructuring Insights" note. Statutory citations: 11 U.S.C. §§ 363, 502, 506, 541, 547, 548, 1129; FRBP 2014, 2019. Plan-confirmation taxonomy (cramdown, absolute priority, gerrymandered classes) used naturally.

**Recurring sub-themes:**
- BlockFills (Bankr. D. Del., 25-XXXXX) — customer commingling, Dominion Capital litigation, cure / contract-rejection calendar
- Legacy cases reaching plan effectuation (Celsius, Genesis, FTX) — distribution mechanics, in-kind vs. fiat
- Hong Kong / Cayman parallel proceedings — Chapter 15 recognition issues
- Stablecoin issuer distress watchlist
- DeFi-as-debtor experiments (Tornado Cash precedent, DAO governance under bankruptcy law)

**Signal test for high urgency:** A new major filing, a customer-property ruling, a § 363 sale order on a major operating business, or a Chapter 15 recognition decision. One major filing = "high"; one significant hearing/order = "normal"; zero qualifying items = "quiet."

**Cross-references:**
- Cross-link Tab 04 (Ponzi / Fraud Recovery) when an insolvency case involves alleged fraud (Goliath Ventures-style fact patterns).
- Cross-link Tab 07 (bankruptcy creditor rights) on any plan-confirmation appellate decision arising from a crypto case.

**Persistent artifact:** `crypto-insolvency/dashboard.html` — internal dashboard listing active matters, key dates, plan-effective dates, and source feed.
