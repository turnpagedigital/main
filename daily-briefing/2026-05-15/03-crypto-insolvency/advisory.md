## State of play

**BlockFills (Bankr. D. Del., filed March 2026).** Chapter 11 filed after a February 2026 liquidity crisis. Reported ~$500M in liabilities against $50–100M in assets, ~$75M in losses. The case was preceded by Dominion Capital obtaining a TRO alleging BlockFills misappropriated customer crypto and failed to return millions in deposits. Customer commingling allegations are central; the court has frozen Bitcoin holdings tied to the firm. Customer-property characterization (segregated vs. estate property) is the determinative issue for unsecured customer recovery rates — and is the through-line from Celsius, FTX, and Genesis.

**Macro recovery trends.** Cleary's restructuring team has noted that several of the older crypto cases (Celsius, Genesis) are now approaching 100% recovery for certain customer claim classes, with some classes reaching 120%. That is a data point worth carrying into BlockFills customer-committee conversations: the precedent for customer-led plans yielding meaningful recoveries is now established, but the asset-segregation findings in the early cases were the determinative variable.
