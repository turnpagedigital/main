## Framing for clients

For Andrew's recovery-counsel clients, the recurring 2026 themes:

1. **Net-winner clawback exposure.** Even good-faith net-winner investors remain exposed to clawback under both actual and constructive fraud theories. Investors taking distributions from any platform currently under SEC or DOJ investigation should preserve records of value-given to support a good-faith / value defense.

2. **Crypto-Ponzi tracing.** Forensic accountants are now routinely able to trace USDT/USDC flows across mixers in receiver investigations; the gating issue is enforcement of receiver orders against foreign exchanges. BVI, Cayman, and Hong Kong cooperation has improved materially; Caribbean shell-jurisdictions remain harder.

3. **The Norton Rose framework.** The Norton Rose Fulbright note (linked below) is the canonical recent summary of "even the winners lose" — i.e., constructive fraud theories sweeping in distributions that exceeded principal investment. Useful for client letters.
