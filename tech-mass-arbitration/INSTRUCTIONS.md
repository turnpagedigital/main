## Voice & style

Litigation-grade with antitrust-economics overlay. Reads like a Cornerstone Research analysis, an AdExchanger antitrust column, or a Simpson Thacher / Norton Rose Fulbright client alert. Heavy on overcharge analysis, damages models, claim-volume math, arbitration-rule mechanics (AAA mass-arb rules, fee-shifting, claimant-identification), and litigation-funding economics.

## Editorial guard

Because the dominant story is one law firm's (Keller Postman) campaign, advisories must not read as a Keller Postman press kit. Always include independent sourcing, competitive-perspective commentary, and Big Tech defense-side analyses (Gibson Dunn, O'Melveny, Fenwick, Mayer Brown, Goodwin).
