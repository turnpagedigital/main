## Active Campaigns

**Google advertiser mass arbitration (Keller Postman).** Ashley Keller of Keller Postman publicly launched the campaign in April 2026, telling Bloomberg his firm had signed up a "significant number" of advertisers and was preparing to file the first claim waves that week. Keller estimates aggregate exposure at **$218 billion or more** based on his retained economist's modeling — built around a 5–10% overcharge on Google ad spend dating back to August 2016, trebled under Clayton Act §4 ([Bloomberg, Apr 13, 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-13/google-faces-mass-arbitration-by-advertisers-seeking-billions); [Claims Journal, Apr 14, 2026](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/04/14/336866.htm); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/advertisers-are-gearing-up-to-hit-google-with-mass-arbitration-claims-worth-billions-474276)). As of May 11, 2026, Keller Postman's intake portal reports it "represents thousands of U.S. businesses" in the campaign ([MassArbitrationClaims.com](https://www.massarbitrationclaims.com/blog/google-mass-arbitration/); [adclaimfiling.com](https://adclaimfiling.com/)). Google Ads' TOS routes claims into **AAA** consumer/commercial arbitration; the campaign deliberately leverages the AAA's post-April 2024 mass-arb supplement.

**Keller Postman track record / timeline benchmark.** Prior Keller Postman campaigns took **12–24 months** from first filings to settlement leverage: DoorDash (6,000+ filed May 2019, compelled Feb 2020); Postmates (10,000+ filed Feb 2020, Ninth Circuit affirmance Sep 2020, $10M fee order); Intuit (~200,000 claimants by March 2022) ([Keller Postman — DoorDash](https://www.kellerpostman.com/keller-postman-represents-5000-doordash-delivery-workers-in-mass-arbitration/); [Postmates](https://www.kellerpostman.com/keller-postman-represents-15000-postmates-couriers-in-mass-arbitration/); [Intuit](https://www.kellerpostman.com/keller-postman-represents-200000-intuit-customers-in-mass-arbitration/)).

**Apple developer-tax track.** No formal Keller-style mass-arb campaign has been announced yet. The dominant US vehicles remain (i) *Cameron v. Apple* developer class action (revived for reconsideration in Dec 2025 after Ninth Circuit set aside Judge Gonzalez Rogers' Oct 2025 dismissal), and (ii) the **Apple consumer iOS antitrust class action** with a February 2026 trial date ([Top Class Actions](https://topclassactions.com/lawsuit-settlements/lawsuit-news/apple-class-action-lawsuit-and-settlement-news/judge-sets-february-2026-trial-date-in-apple-antitrust-class-action/); [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/12/19/court-will-reconsider-app-store-class-action-case-worth-billions); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/12/18/apple-faces-revived-app-store-antitrust-class-action/)). The *Epic v. Apple* contempt order — left in place after SCOTUS denied Apple's stay request in May 2026 — strengthens any future developer mass-arb posture by establishing willful anti-steering conduct ([SCOTUSblog, May 2026](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/05/court-tuns-down-apples-request-to-pause-order-holding-it-in-contempt/)).

**Meta advertiser track.** *DZ Reserve v. Facebook* (long-running advertiser class action over inflated potential-reach metrics, est. damages $2.4–6.2B) is nearing trial. Meta's August 2025 motion to compel arbitration of post-2018 ad buyers was denied in December 2025 for waiver; Meta has filed for Ninth Circuit review, and counsel report **over 100,000 individual arbitration claims have been filed in parallel since late 2024** ([Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/meta-stab-at-arbitration-misses-in-long-running-class-action-over-ad-reach/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/413951/)).

**Amazon seller track.** Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement contains a forced-arbitration clause, which has so far prevented seller class actions. The consumer-side antitrust class action (*De Coster*, ~288M class members) was pushed from October 2026 to June 2027 ([Hagens Berman](https://www.hbsslaw.com/cases/amazon-com-antitrust-de-coster); [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2398054/amazon-gets-massive-antitrust-class-action-trial-delayed)). Keller Postman's case page also flags Amazon as a target vertical ([Keller Postman — Amazon](https://www.kellerpostman.com/cases/amazon-antitrust-lawsuits/)).

## Underlying Antitrust Posture

**Search monopolization — *U.S. v. Google* (D.D.C., Judge Mehta).** August 5, 2024 liability ruling: Google violated Sherman §2 in "general search services" and "general search text advertising" via exclusive-default deals with Apple, Samsung, Verizon, and others ([Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2024/08/alerts-technology-antc-google-is-an-illegal-monopoly-federal-court-rules); [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-our-thinking/landmark-decision-dc-federal-court-holds-google-maintained-illegal-monopoly)). **Remedies ruling Sep 2, 2025**: Mehta declined Chrome divestiture, but ordered an end to exclusive-default contracts, mandated certain search-index/user-interaction data sharing with qualified competitors, and imposed a 6-year technical committee ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/09/02/google-antitrust-search-ruling.html); [Hughes Hubbard](https://www.hugheshubbard.com/news/court-issues-remedies-ruling-in-united-states-v-google-search-case); [DOJ Press Release](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-wins-significant-remedies-against-google)). Google filed Notice of Appeal **Jan 16, 2026**; DOJ cross-appealed seeking stronger divestitures by its **Feb 3, 2026** deadline ([tech-insider.org](https://tech-insider.org/google-antitrust-appeal-doj-search-monopoly-2026/)).

**Ad tech monopolization — *U.S. v. Google* (E.D. Va., Judge Brinkema).** **April 17, 2025** liability ruling: Google unlawfully monopolized the open-web publisher ad-server market and open-web ad-exchange market, and unlawfully tied DFP to AdX. The advertiser ad-networks count was dismissed for lack of a defined relevant market ([Simpson Thacher](https://www.stblaw.com/about-us/publications/view/2025/04/25/district-court-rules-google-is-a-monopolist-in-ad-tech); [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/platforms/google-is-found-guilty-of-operating-an-ad-tech-monopoly/); [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/programmatic/google-monopoly-power-leonie-brinkema-ruling/)). Remedies trial September–November 2025; closing arguments **Nov 21, 2025**. Brinkema indicated she had begun drafting and signaled skepticism about full AdX divestiture on timing grounds. **Most observers expect a final remedies order in mid-2026** ([Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/cb2c3ded/what-you-need-to-know-from-closing-arguments-in-us-v-google); [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/doj-google-adtech-monopoly-remedies-trial-breakup/); [The Current](https://www.thecurrent.com/data-privacy-judge-structural-remedies-google-ad-tech-monopoly-case)).

## AAA / JAMS Mechanics

**AAA Supplementary Rules for Multiple Case Filings (effective April 1, 2024).** Trigger: **25+ similar claims** filed against the same or related parties with coordinated counsel, even if filed serially. April 2024 expansion brought B2B and construction disputes within the rules, but those non-consumer/employment categories have a **100-claim** threshold ([ADR.org](https://go.adr.org/mass-arbitration-rules-april2024.html); [Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2024/01/alerts-otherindustries-updated-aaa-rules-and-fees-could-change); [ABA Business Law Today](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/business_law/resources/business-law-today/2025-march/evolution-of-mass-arbitration/)). Note: the Google advertiser campaign involves business claimants, so the 100-claim threshold likely applies — material for defense strategy.

**Fees.** AAA now uses a single initiation fee plus a smaller per-case post-initiation fee. Consumer/employment: $3,125 per claimant side / $8,125 per business side, flat regardless of size ([Fenwick](https://www.fenwick.com/insights/publications/aaa-issues-updates-to-consumer-mass-arbitration-rules-and-fee-schedule)).

**Process Arbitrator / Global Mediator.** Both providers appoint a preliminary decision-maker at intake to handle threshold issues before merits arbitrators are seated.

**Counsel verification.** AAA and JAMS both now require claimant counsel to affirm the accuracy of each claimant's information under Rule-11-style sanction risk ([Privacy World](https://www.privacyworld.blog/2024/03/updates-to-mass-arbitration-rules-scope-and-anticipated-impact/)).

**JAMS Mass Arbitration Procedures (May 2024).** JAMS defines a mass arbitration as **75+** coordinated demands, with a $7,500 flat initiation fee regardless of case count ([JAMS](https://www.jamsadr.com/mass-arbitration-procedures); [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2024/05/jams-adopts-new-mass-arbitration-rules-and-fee-schedules)).

## Damages Modeling

**The overcharge theory.** Plaintiff economist (unnamed in public coverage) models a counterfactual world where Google's tying of DFP/AdX and self-preferencing bidding (Project Bernanke, dynamic revenue share) did not exist. The methodology produces an estimated **5–10% overcharge on advertiser ad spend** in the relevant period (August 2016 onward) ([AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/antitrust/for-google-advertisers-who-overpaid-the-monopoly-dont-hate-arbitrate/); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/if-you-bought-google-ads-from-august-2016-to-the-present-you-may-be-eligible-to-seek-compensation-through-a-new-arbitration-program-302763792.html)).

**Trebling math.** Section 4 Clayton Act: proven overcharge × 3 + fees/costs. For an advertiser with $100M of historical Google Ads spend, max claim is roughly $30M (10% × 3) ([eMarketing Association](https://www.emarketingassociation.com/about-the-emarketing-association-ema/sponsors/the-google-ads-spend-recovery-opportunity-my-pov/)).

**Issue preclusion exposure.** Winston & Strawn flagged "Google's $2.3M Mistake" — Google's bench-trial strategy in liability phases created collateral estoppel risk in downstream private cases. The DOJ liability findings can be invoked as preclusive in subsequent advertiser/publisher proceedings ([Winston & Strawn](https://www.winston.com/en/blogs-and-podcasts/competition-corner/googles-dollar23-million-mistake-how-a-bench-trial-play-became-a-preclusion-problem)).

## Defense Posture

**The Heckman cloud.** *Heckman v. Live Nation* (9th Cir. 2024) invalidated Live Nation/Ticketmaster's specifically anti-mass-arb clauses as procedurally/substantively unconscionable. Live Nation filed cert May 5, 2025; if SCOTUS reverses, defendants gain wider latitude to design mass-arb-specific procedures (batching, bellwethers, capped fees) ([Jackson Lewis](https://www.jacksonlewis.com/insights/mass-arbitration-rules-under-scrutiny-live-nation-asks-scotus-overturn-heckman); [O'Melveny](https://www.omm.com/insights/alerts-publications/mass-arbitrations-in-2025-key-legal-shifts-every-company-should-know/); [Gibson Dunn Jan 2026 webcast](https://www.gibsondunn.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/WebcastSlides-Arbitration-Agreement-Enforceability-and-Mass-Arbitration-29-JAN-2026.pdf)).

**California SB 707 + Hohenshelt.** California Supreme Court in *Hohenshelt v. Superior Court* (Aug 2025) held SB 707 is **not** FAA-preempted, but softened its 30-day fee-payment deadline — late payment can be excused absent willfulness/fraud/gross negligence ([Privacy World — 2025 YIR](https://www.privacyworld.blog/2025/12/2025-mass-arbitration-year-in-review/)).

**Waiver attacks.** Meta's December 2025 loss on its eleventh-hour motion to compel arbitration of long-pending advertiser claims is the dominant cautionary tale on waiver.

**Corporate-plaintiff novelty.** Most mass-arb doctrine developed in consumer/employee contexts. The Google campaign is the first major **B2B / corporate-plaintiff** mass arb at scale, raising open questions on unconscionability (less sympathetic claimants), TOS clickwrap enforceability against sophisticated advertisers, and whether AAA's 100-claim B2B threshold applies in lieu of the consumer 25-claim trigger.

## Publisher Class Action (Parallel, Separate from Advertiser Mass Arb)

Distinct from the advertiser mass arb because publishers are *not* bound by Google's advertiser TOS arbitration clause. In *In re Google Digital Advertising Antitrust Litigation* (S.D.N.Y., Judge P. Kevin Castel) the court:
- **Oct 2025**: partial summary judgment for Gannett, Daily Mail, and Inform on liability (Castel applied collateral estoppel from Brinkema's Apr 2025 ruling) ([Courthouse News PDF](https://www.courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/google-antitrust-mdl-summary-judgment-order.pdf); [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2404555/google-liable-again-as-doj-s-ad-tech-win-extends-to-mdl)).
- **Dec 15, 2025**: certified a class of ~5,000 publishers (Dec 15, 2016 – Mar 31, 2024) seeking $1.7B+ ([Law.com](https://www.law.com/newyorklawjournal/2025/12/15/us-judge-grants-class-certification-in-google-ad-tech-mdl-case/); [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/tech-and-telecom-law/publisher-plaintiffs-get-class-status-in-google-ad-exchange-case)).
- **Early 2026**: notice plan approved; 45-day opt-out window from first notice ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/a-running-list-of-publisher-lawsuits-targeting-googles-ad-tech-practices/)).
