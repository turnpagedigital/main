**On the Bartz claim specifically**, we offer three structures depending on what fits your treasury, accounting, and risk posture:

- **Forward purchase** — we buy all or part of your claim outright at a fixed price. You take cash today; we take settlement risk, timing risk, and the administrative burden of filing and chasing the distribution.
- **Non-recourse advance** — capital today against your expected recovery, repaid only if and when the settlement pays. No claim sale, no balance-sheet liability.
- **Hybrid** — partial liquidity now with retained upside on the tail. Useful if you're confident in the floor but want to preserve participation above it.
