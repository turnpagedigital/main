No whitelisted headlines surfaced from the live news scan covering any fraud-recovery, receivership, or Ponzi-adjacent enforcement development in the past 24 hours.

**Goliath — standing posture for context.** The case remains the desk's most active near-term watch item. 
A civil forfeiture complaint has been filed against 7 real properties and 11 vehicles allegedly purchased by Christopher Delgado, who operated Goliath as a Ponzi scheme from January 2023 through January 2026.
 
Court filings state that the company reported assets of between $1M and $10M against liabilities estimated at between $100M and $500M.
 
As of the receiver's last reported figure, approximately $366,000 has been recovered — a very small fraction of the money allegedly lost.
 The July 28 Disclosure of Compensation (Dkt. 22) is a routine professional-retention filing; it signals counsel are positioning for fee applications but carries no distribution or asset-realization implication on its own.

**Power Block Coin — complaint filed July 23.** 
The Official Committee of Unsecured Creditors proposed a liquidating plan addressing over $192 million in claims and establishing an independent Liquidating Trust to manage assets and potential legal claims.
 The July 23 adversary complaint (Dkt. 532) has not been publicly described in verified sources retrieved this run; its filing is consistent with the committee pursuing derivative standing to assert avoidance or recovery claims, which was the subject of a contested motion before Judge Parker as recently as March 2026. 
The Celsius-related objecting creditors have asserted over $133 million in preference claims against the debtor in connection with the pending Celsius Network LLC et al. bankruptcy (Case No. 22-10964, Bankr. S.D.N.Y.).
 The interplay between that preference exposure and whatever claims the adversary complaint asserts is the key issue to track when the complaint becomes publicly available.

**Madoff / BLMIS — no activity in 24-hour window; prior-cycle reference.** The seventeenth distribution closed earlier this year. 
The SIPA Trustee distributed more than $253 million on a pro rata basis, bringing the cumulative payout to nearly $15.38 billion, representing approximately 72.848% of allowed claims.
 
As of July 24, 2026, SIPC has committed approximately $850.9 million to the BLMIS liquidation.
 The next distribution motion will turn on litigation recoveries accumulated since January 31, 2026; no filing has been made.

The next scheduled milestone to watch across tracked matters: any public docketing of the Power Block Coin adversary complaint details (Dkt. 532, filed July 23), which will determine whether avoidance exposure for net-winner transferees becomes quantifiable.
