# Bartz v. Anthropic Briefing — August 8, 2026

The sole development in the 72-hour window is a class-counsel-sourced update published yesterday by the Textbook & Academic Authors Association. 
Class counsel confirmed that class members will not begin receiving payments until at least the end of 2026, with disbursement contingent on whether any appeals are filed before the August 19 deadline.
 That deadline — 11 days out — is now the controlling variable for the entire distribution timeline. 
Payments will be made in two stages, roughly 70% in the first stage and 30% in the second, reflecting Anthropic's installment payment structure running through September 2027.
 The practical implication is that even in the no-appeal scenario, claimants are looking at a partial recovery in late 2026 and a residual tail payment that tracks Anthropic's funding calendar, not a single lump disbursement.

The August 19 appeal deadline is the nearest binary event. 
If no appeals are filed, class counsel indicated that an online portal will be launched by the end of August containing claimants' consolidated claim forms
 — the administrative precursor to actual disbursement. Any timely appeal would halt that sequence and push payments into an indeterminate window governed by appellate scheduling. The identity and standing of a potential appellant remains the relevant uncertainty: 
Judge Martínez-Olguín overruled all 53 objections and recorded 350 valid opt-outs representing 1,802 works, or 0.03% of the total
 — a thin objector pool, though standing to appeal is not limited to those who formally objected at the trial-court level. The Passman late opt-out application (Dkt. 676), opposed by both class counsel (Dkt. 678) and Anthropic (Dkt. 679) and disposed of in the July 20 final approval order (Dkt. 680), is the most obvious candidate for appellate review, but whether Passman's counsel has filed or intends to file a notice of appeal is unconfirmed as of this writing; confirmation would require a notice of appeal appearing on the docket.

Today's update is procedural in character — a payment-timeline clarification from class counsel, not a docket filing or judicial action. It does not alter the recovery math or the settlement structure. The next concrete milestone is August 19: if the docket is clean at close of business that day, the portal launch and late-2026 first-stage disbursement become the operative working assumption. The subsequent hard date is September 25, 2026, when Anthropic's third installment payment is due, bringing the funded total to $1.05 billion of the $1.5 billion obligation.
