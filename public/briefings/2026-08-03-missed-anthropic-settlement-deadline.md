# Missed the Anthropic Settlement Deadline? Here's What You Can Still Do

The short answer: the claims deadline was **March 30, 2026**, and it has not been extended.

But "missed the deadline" doesn't always mean "gets nothing." The Plan of Allocation schedules checks to **non-filing claimants on August 21, 2026**, and the settlement's release was deliberately written to leave several doors open — including all claims about Anthropic's *outputs* and anything it did after August 25, 2025. Here's the map.

## First: are you actually in the class?

The settlement covers copyright owners of the **482,460 books on the Works List** — works with an ISBN or ASIN that Anthropic downloaded from the pirate libraries LibGen and PiLiMi, registered with the U.S. Copyright Office within five years of publication. 91.3% of those works were claimed by the deadline. If your book isn't on the Works List at all, you were never in this settlement — and nothing about it releases your own claims (more below).

## If you're in the class but didn't file

- **You're still bound by the settlement** (unless you opted out by the deadline — only 350 people did), meaning you released the past-conduct claims it covers.
- **You may still receive something.** Under the Plan of Allocation, the administrator sends checks to non-filing claimants on August 21, 2026. The mechanics and amounts are the administrator's to determine — watch your mail, and make sure the administrator has a current address for you.
- **Be wary of anyone charging you to "recover" a missed claim.** Verify anything you're told against the official administrator's site.

## What the settlement did NOT take from you

This is the part most coverage skips. The release covers **only Anthropic's past conduct through August 25, 2025** — the acquisition and copying of pirated books. It explicitly does **not** release:

- **Output claims** — if Claude reproduces or closely imitates your work, that's an open theory, settlement or no settlement
- **Future conduct** — anything Anthropic does after August 25, 2025
- **Claims against everyone else** — OpenAI, Meta, Stability, Midjourney, Suno. The [OpenAI MDL is heading toward trial](/briefings/2026-08-03-openai-copyright-mdl-authors), and [Meta's piracy claims survived](/briefings/2026-08-03-ai-copyright-lawsuit-tracker) even after its fair-use win.

Opted out? You're in different — and potentially stronger — territory: the *Chicken Soup for the Soul* and Carreyrou suits show the individual-action path is live. That's a conversation for a copyright litigator.
