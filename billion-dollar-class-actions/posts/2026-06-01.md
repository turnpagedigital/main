# $1B+ Class Actions & Mass Arb — Posts for Monday, June 1, 2026

## LinkedIn

If you have a Roundup claim, June 4 is a hard fork — and it's three days away.

That's the opt-out deadline in Bayer's proposed $7.25 billion Roundup class settlement before Judge Chhabria (N.D. Cal.), ahead of the July 9 fairness hearing. Once the window closes, the choice is made for you: stay in, and you accept the class framework with its roughly $165,000 per-claimant ceiling based on disease and age; opt out, and you keep the right to prosecute individually — and you give up the settlement's certainty and speed.

The math is personal. A claimant with severe injury, strong causation evidence, and a damages model well above $165,000 may rationally opt out. A claimant who values certainty, or whose individual case is harder to prove, may rationally stay in. What no one should do is let the deadline decide by default.

This is the nearest of four June pivots stacking up: Visa/Mastercard interchange (MDL 1720) goes to a final-approval hearing June 17; Keller Postman's $218 billion Google advertiser mass arbitration opens its first AAA wave June 23; and Bartz v. Anthropic's $1.5 billion copyright fund has per-work distributions due June 11. Different defendants, same recovery posture — and the same lesson: the calendar is doing the work, so run your own numbers before the dates run you.

If you're a Roundup class member, review your individual damages model against the $165,000 ceiling this week. After June 4, that option is gone.
